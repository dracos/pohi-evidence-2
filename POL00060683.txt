Legally privileged and confidential

1. if a person thinks they have been wrongly convicted they can seek to apply for permission to leave to appeal ~

they do this by going to the Crown Court

if leave is given they can then take their case to the Court of Appeal

Def has to show that new evidence exists which casts doubt on the conviction

Court of Appeal then review the case

They look to see what the defendant was convicted on — e.g. was it only on the evidence that is now being called

in to question OR was there other evidence as well. If there is other evidence as well then the Court of Appeal

may very well let the conviction stand, even in the light of new evidence undermining the other original

evidence.

6. Court of Appeal may quash the conviction. They can also order a retrial. If they don’t order a retrial, we can’t
ask for one. If they do, we can say we don’t want one.

ww

After that you are also locking at civil actions against us for wrongful prosecution (if the conviction is quashed I
guess).
