MONDAY 29 NOVEMBER 2010

Nicholas Brown (Newcastle upon Tyne East) : To ask the Secretary of
State, Department for Business, Innovation and Skills, what recent
assessment he has made of the performance of the Horizon computer
programme; and what (a) errors relating to the reconciliation of accounts were
reported to his Department and (b) remedial action was taken in the latest
period for which figures are available.(26688)

Edward Davey

The performance of the Horizon computer system is an operational matter for
Post Office Ltd.

I have therefore asked Paula Vennells, the managing director of Post Office
Ltd, to respond directly to the Rt Hon. Member and a copy of her reply will be
placed in the libraries of the House.
