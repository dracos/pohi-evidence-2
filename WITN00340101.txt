CWU MEMBERS’ SURVEY ~ RESULTS

Further to my letter of 16" March 2017, I am sharing with you the results of our
membership survey which closed today, 31% March. I hope you respect the results of
this survey as a genuine outcome as our members have participated with honesty
and of their own free will and volition.

No doubt you will be disappointed with the results overleaf, especially as they can
only be interpreted as being highly critical of the overall direction you and your Board
are taking the Post Office in. I take no pleasure whatsoever in the fact the survey
results confirm the morale of our members is at rock bottom and they have no real
hope for the future.

The survey form was designed to enable our members to provide general comments
on how they feel about working for the Post Office. We have received numerous
contributions in this regard which we obviously need to carefully consider and
analyse. Accordingly I will advise you of the nature and recurring themes of the
comments at a later date.

Evidently the results are extremely serious; the Union will therefore provide the
results of the survey to Margot James, Government Minister responsible for the Post
Office, highlighting our real concerns over the general direction of the Post Office. I
will also be informing all Post Office Board members of the survey results so they are
aware of the views of our members.

Given an astonishing 92% have no confidence in the Post Office Board’s leadership I
am calling upon you to immediately abandon your franchising plans including those
Crowns for which you have yet to announce a partner. I also invite you to confirm
that the Supply Chain operation will remain in house (not be outsourced) for the next
5 years. A positive response on both of these crucial issues will help considerably to
address the most worrying response to the survey in respect of the lack of job
security.

General Secretary: Dave Ward
