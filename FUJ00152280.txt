Hi Nicola,

I'm not sure if there is some confusion around our two main systems. The failure on Friday was Triole for Service
which is our call management system and had no effect on the telephony system (Commander). We reverted to
manual call logging which had no effect on the live service as the outage was short. I believe that the issue was due to
the Apache Tomcat web service stopping. This was restarted without a re-boot to the server.

This issue has been escalated to Computer Associates for investigation. While the outage was minimal, we have seen
some glitches with the Apache Tomcat service and the application is on amber alert. (l.e. we are taking any outage
seriously!).

Recently more Triole servers have been added to the estate to improve performance further.

With regards to telephony, we have an upgrade to the system this Saturday (after desk close) to enable “multimode”
function. This effectively adds even more resilience than we have now and means that if our main server fails on the
Stevenage site, the system will seamlessly switch over to a server on another site. (We have multiple sites on
Commander).

All systems can have failures, and both Commander and Triole are relatively new in the business. We are committed
however to providing a world class infrastructure solution to our clients and continue to work towards this with ongoing
improvements.

I hope this allays some of your concerns.
