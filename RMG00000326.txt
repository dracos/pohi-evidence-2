Long Term Incentive Plan
6 The Long Term Incentive Plan matures this year. The Return on Total
Assets outturn is 11.3% against the following targets:
Threshold: 8.2%
On-target 8.57%
Stretch 11.03%
This results in a Company Award of 37.5% of base salary.
The 3 year ROTA result is 26.0% against the following targets:
Threshold: 17.4%
On-target 19.3%
Stretch 29.7%

This results in a final multiplier of 1.75.
