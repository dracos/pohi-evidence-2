97/80 CHIEF EXECUTIVES PROGRAMME

Fiona explained a new initiative to have senior
Managers "fix" business critical issues in ICL
within a Development framework. It was proposed
that the first Chief Executive’s programme would be
launched on 5, 6, 7 October 1997

Participants would be selected from the senior
Management population, from all businesses and

functions. Projects would be "sponsored" by
members of the EMC with Keith leading the
programme.

The workshop briefing at the start of the programme
would last two days (6/7 October). Teams would
then work on the project for a 60-70 day period of
time. They would be expected to use technology
effectively in their project work. Participants
would continue with all their normal duties and a
programme like this would take up 20% additional

time.
ALL EMC members were asked to give feedback on the
participants list and on the proposed projects to
Fiona.
