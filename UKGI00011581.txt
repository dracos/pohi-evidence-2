Meeting with Nick Read (CEO) & Tim Parker (Chair)
of Post Office Limited

11.00-11.30am, Monday 16" March

External attendees:
Nick Read, CEO, & Tim Parker, Chair, Post Office Limited (POL)

Internal Attendees:
Carl Creswell, Director, PBS, Retail and Post
Tom Cooper, Director, UKGI Post Office Shareholder team

Purpose of meeting and Handling:
The objectives of this meeting are to:

1. Allow a formal introduction between yourself and two key senior stakeholders
within your portfolio.

2. Discuss live issues, including increasing parliamentary scrutiny, upcoming
Select Committee hearing and ‘Independent Review’; along with people and
planning.

3. Discuss POL’s strategy ahead of the Spending Review - POL to update you on
their 4 Year Plan (4YP), Purpose, Strategy and Growth work and progress on
Spending Review case, working with BEIS and UKGI, and press the need for
POL to move forward quickly and be responsive given the SR will conclude by

July.
Agenda:
No Item Time
1.__I Live issues 11.00 - 11.20
2. POL’s strategy ahead of the Spending Review 11.20 - 11.30

Points to make:
1. Live Issues
POL’s implementation of commitments following Horizon settlement

e Acknowledge that when you spoke to Nick on 4 March you touched on steps
POL were taking following the settlement. Say that — whilst it is positive that
POL has taken operational steps following the judgments to increase
postmaster support, improve processes and bolster training— it is important that
Nick continues the drive to re-establish a more productive relationship with
postmasters.

o Acknowledge the high level of Parliamentary interest in the Post Office
at the moment.

o Ask Nick about progress on their post-settlement actions, particularly on
the historic shortfalls scheme that we understand will be announced by
POL on 24 March. The scheme was discussed at the 10 March Select
Committee hearing. It is important this scheme is transparent, and that
POL communicate openly and honestly with postmasters in relation to
any developments.

o Thank Nick for being proactive in engaging with the CCRC and ask
about POL’s progress on assessing previous prosecutions, his views on
next steps and potential implications.

o Ask Nick about POL’s plans to prioritise culture change and
demonstrate how they will communicate the lessons they have learnt
with postmasters and the public.

BEIS Select Committee hearing on Horizon on 24 March

e Note that the previous points will be raised at the hearing, with a strong focus
on the lessons POL and Government have learnt from the Horizon scandal.

o Ask Nick about the key points he plans to make at the hearing and any
areas of concern that he has.

o Encourage Nick and his team to engage with BEIS and UKGI in
preparation. You may wish to suggest a rehearsal session with
BEIS/UKGI colleagues.

Developments on Independent Review

e POL may seek an update from you. Nick was originally concerned about the
idea of a forward-looking inquiry ‘marking his homework’ so early in his tenure,
but BEIS officials have explained our rationale. This will have been covered in
the meeting between Nick and the Perm Sec on 12 March. If POL press on
this, you may want to:

and this

o Mention that, following No.10’s steer, we are committed to establishing
an independent review to ensure that the Post Office has learned the
necessary lessons and to provide an external assessment of its work to
rebuild its relationship with its postmasters.

o Say that we will need to secure Collective Cabinet Agreement before
any further details are announced.

o Emphasise that we will obviously want to ensure that any review does
not undermine the CCRC’s work, the separate Director of Public
Prosecution’s consideration or the BEIS Select Committee’s Inquiry on
issues emerging from the Horizon IT Court cases.

e At the Board discussion on 10 March, POL discussed the need to seek some
guidance on what services they would need to provide in specific scenarios
e.g. cash distribution in a lock-down scenario. Welcome POL’s input into the
department's assessment of COVID readiness and encourage them to
continue discussions with the BEIS policy team and wider OGDs — especially
HMT and DWP to ensure potential restrictions on cash do not negatively
impact vulnerable customers — to keep abreast of the latest guidance.

2. POL’s 4YP, Purpose, Strategy and Growth and strategy ahead of the
Spending Review

e Acknowledge that POL is still in the process of bringing its Purpose, Strategy and
Growth (PSG) work that has been supported by McKinsey into a detailed 4 Year
Plan (4YP) to March 2024.

o Ask POL to update you on the process so far on the development of PSG
and the 4YP.

o Note that the POL Board on 10 March discussed the issues with putting
together a budget given the uncertainties this year. Ask for POL’s views on
this.

o Ask Nick to also update you on obstacles to overcome before approval at
the next POL Board.

e To be aware after the POL Board on 10 March the possibility of Al Cameron’s
(Chief Financial Officer) departure was discussed. You may want to probe for an
update on this.

Spending Review

e Say that you are pleased to hear that POL are working closely with BEIS and
UKGI on developing a robust Spending Review case.

o Mention that you are aware that there will be clear pushback from HMT on
a continued £50m subsidy for POL, so POL need to be able to have a
better and more detailed narrative in order to support their case.

o Reiterate that the POL data received so far has been very helpful but to
develop a persuasive SR case for the subsidy we will need more on
evidence of the likely impacts of their proposals and on changes in market
conditions since POL’s last funding was agreed. Acknowledge that POL’s
Finance team are working on delivering this.

Relevant context:

Alex Chisholm met with Nick Read on Thursday 12 March covering live issues
including parliamentary scrutiny and commitments around the litigation, POL’s PSG,
4YP and Spending Review case, the Post Office card account (POca) and POL’s
bonuses. We also attach a copy of a letter that Alex Chisholm sent to Tim Parker on
26 February, as part of the Department’s annual process to outline our expectations
of POL, as provided for in the agreed Framework Document.

Nick Read - Chief Executive. Joined the Board in September 2019

Nick joined Post Office as Chief Executive Officer in September 2019. Nick was
most recently Group CEO at Extra Energy and prior to that CEO at Nisa Retail
Limited where he led the stabilisation and transformation of the business ahead of
its sale to the Co-Op in 2018.

Nick has over 25 years of consumer and commercial experience and has previously
held senior roles at Tesco, Vodafone, HBOS, Lloyds Banking Group and Thomas
Cook. In his earlier career, Nick was a Consumer and Retail Strategy Manager at
Deloitte Consulting, a Purchasing Director for Aldi UK and spent 4 years as Captain
of the 4*/7» Royal Dragoon Guard, H.M. Forces.

Tim Parker — Independent Chair, Post Office Limited

Tim Parker has been Independent Chair of POL since October 2015 and is also
Chair of POL’s Nominations Committee and a member of its Remuneration
Committee. Tim has been CEO of several well-known companies: Kenwood, Clarks,
Kwik-Fit, the AA and Samsonite. He has served as a Non-Executive Director on
several FTSE 100 companies: Legal and General, Alliance Boots and Compass
Group, and has been a Board member of the Audit Commission and the South West
Regional Development Agency. He has been an Industrial Partner at CVC Capital
Partners. Tim is the Executive Chairman of Samsonite Corporation and Non-
Executive Chairman of the National Trust.
