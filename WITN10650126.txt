There is one more piece to this jigsaw.
I'm not sure if you are aware of this already, but there is a separate claim running in the employment tribunal by 123
SPMs seeking worker status. This claim is in its infancy, with the first case management hearing scheduled for early
June. Some of the tribunal Claimants overlap with the Group Litigation claimants but otherwise they are separate
actions. The tribunal claim is, we think, being funded by the Communication Workers Union as part of a broader
campaign to obtain recognition as a union for SPMs. A different law firm is acting for the tribunal Claimants,
Pennington Manches.

The Common Issues judgment has some crossover effect on the tribunal claim. Post Office has instructed Chris Jeans
QC on the tribunal claim and he has recently produced two advice notes (attached) on how the Cl judgment impacts
on the tribunal claim. I've attached all his advice notes to date:
* The first advice note is a merits opinion, which also has useful background information.
* The second advice note explains why the Cl judgment effectively prevents Post Office from striking out the
tribunal claim (which was our proposed strategy before the judgment).
* The third advice note sets out the areas of the Cl judgment that CJQC believes would be useful to appeal
from a "workers' rights" perspective.

For the purposes of next week, it is the third advice note that needs consideration. In summary, CJQC believes that
there are some areas that could be usefully appealed, but if the appeal is unsuccessful and the C of A endorses the
Common Issues findings then that increases the risk that the tribunal will follow those findings.

I don't believe that this changes our appeal strategy but wanted you to be aware of this other angle in case it comes up
at the board meeting

Kind regards

Andy
