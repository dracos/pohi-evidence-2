2 SEPTEMBER REVIEW OF SERVICE

2.1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Within the Fujitsu Services domain for September 06 there were no Major Incidents and one service affecting incident relating to the IPStream
network. All transactions were completed on retries and remedial action has been completed to address this issue.

There were no incidents affecting VIP sites or outside the Fujitsu Services domain in the month.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.76% and 99.36% respectively. OBC targets were also exceeded with all OLAs
met, and the OBC volumes reduced slightly on the August figures to 268.

High volumes of incoming calls continue but the levels are now relatively stable, and this is subject to close management to ensure that any
consequent issues are contained. Counter Printer calls are still the highest volumes. The Engineering SLTs are still giving cause for concern, but
agreed remedial action plans are in place, and resource and spares planning is continuing on the basis of the current levels being sustained into the
peak trading period in November / December.

Network performance levels have exceeded targets, and the number of network related calls remains steady.

Service Level reporting in September reflects the new contractual requirements. The details of these will be reviewed and refined for the October
reporting.

A total of 94 complaints were received, and 56 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, and 23 were for investigation and comment. These
levels reflect the agreed process for improved visibility of all complaints. Of the 3 justified complaints, 2 related to engineering and 1 to Counter
Printer performance.

2.1.2. OBC Branch Change Service Summary

In September 2006 there were a total of 268 changes undertaken, 5 of these were cancelled changes. With the exclusion of ‘Closures’ 230 changes
were undertaken. Fujitsu were responsible for 6.5 change requests not being completed on the target date. For Fujitsu this represents a failure rate of
1.09% against the new 4% OLA minus Closures. It also represents a failure rate of 2.47% against the original target of 5%. There were 4 line plant
failures; this represents a failure rate of 0.95% against the old 4% target excluding Line Plant. This results in an improvement against the August
figures of 2.27% for the target without Closures. Compared to the August calculations, an improvement of 0.53% against the 5% target rate and an
improvement of 2.05% against the 4% target rate; excluding line plant.

Post Office Ltd were responsible for 5.5 change requests not being completed on the target date, which represents 2.09% against the 4% target, this
shows a difference against the August calculations of -1.42%.

2.1.4 Business Continuity

During September operational tests were successfully completed for EDS (test 24), the VPN and external firewall elements of the LAN test (sub-
tests 6.3 and 6.4), and the application server and SAN fail-over elements of the POL-FS service (sub-tests 28.2, 28.3 and 28.5). Procedural walk-
throughs were also successfully conducted for the loss of BRAOI (test 1), the NBX client interfaces (test 22) and the Network Banking Service (test
16).

Some progress has been made on revising the Business Continuity plans for the inclusion of the NS&I warrant encashment and MoneyGram
services. In addition progress has been made on the implementation of the LEW02 disaster recovery site.

2.1.5 Message Broadcast

There were 20 MBS sent in September.

2.1.6 Horizon Service Desk and Complaints

In September 94 complaints were passed to POL Customer Relations, 15 of which were excluded as chaser / duplicate calls, resulting in 79
complaints logged against Fujitsu Services, representing a reduction of 18 complaints when compared to August’s complaints data. The top three
areas of complaint were:

Engineer complaints (including response times)
On Line Services being unavailable
Counter Printer problems.

56 of the 79 complaints were passed to Fujitsu Services for information only, and 23 were passed for investigation and comment, a significant
reduction on August’s figure of 40. Of those investigated by Fujitsu Services, 3 were justified complaints (compared to 9 in August) (2 against
engineering (1 including comments against the HSD) & 1 Counter Printer Issue. The justified complaints are detailed in the table below.

Engineering Service / Counter Printers: As the figures above demonstrate, the actions & service improvements implemented through July-
September have resulted in improved service to the branches, and a subsequent reduction in the number of complaints logged at the NBSC. Work is
continuing with engineering to improve the management & information available around engineer arrival time. A section of the engineering desk
has been allocated to deal solely with the A priority calls and the open A priority calls are discussed every hour between the 2 desks. The reduction
in the number of open engineering calls has improved the reliability of engineering provided estimated time of arrival, and this information is passed
to the desk and communicated to the Post Masters. We are continuing to monitor the number of missed visits and have seen volumes continue to
drop throughout September.

4.3 KEY CROSS DOMAIN PROBLEM UPDATE

The RAG status for the key cross domain problems are based on the criteria —

Business Impact BI score Root Cause RC

score

High (H) >20 outlets affected 3 Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place 3

Medium (M) >1 <20 outlets affected 2 Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be 2
agreed or workaround in place

Low (L) <1 outlet affected 1 Fix implemented and problem being monitored 1

RAG status = BI score x RC score = (RED = 6-9) (AMBER = 3-6) (GREEN = 1-2)

The differentiator to be applied between a RED & AMBER RAG status score of 6 depends on whether the RC is known and whether a
workaround is in place i.e. if a the score of 6 is derived by a “Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place = RC score
of 3” then the RAG will be red.

If the RC score was derived by “Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed or a workaround in place = RC
score of 2 then the RAG will be amber.
