Conclusions (1) the big picture

Evidence of some improvement in outlook on remuneration — but some way to go yet

Subpostmasters more negative than other SMEs in terms of growth prospects

Positivity and progress linked to behaviours associated with improving the business — e.g.

+ Engaging with and seeking support from the NFSP

* Marketing the business online

* Upholding standards — e.g. via being part of a symbol group and/or interest in mails accreditation

Serious concerns about the relationship between subpostmasters and Post Office Ltd
