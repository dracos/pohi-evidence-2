Felstead’s appeal to the Court of Appeal, that was eventually allowed and her conviction was
quashed in April 2021. I ceased to have any involvement in December 2020.

Mr Turner is to my mind a very experienced and capable expert witness. Tam confident that had
he been called at Miss Felstead’s trial she would not have been convicted of theft (or any other
offence).

Whaat is perhaps most remarkable, is that Mr Turner retains his original papers from Miss
Felstead’s prosecution, which T have seen.
