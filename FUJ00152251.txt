2 NOVEMBER REVIEW OF SERVICE

2.1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

November resulted another good month for Horizon service provision by Fujitsu will very Service Level Target being met with the
exception of Average Downtime Call to fix which achieved 25.7% against a target of 24.5%. This was due to a seasonal increase in
hardware fault calls of 12%. Despite this significant increase, all engineering and service desk service levels have been met and exceeded.

However, although the SLT performance is creditable, there have been 4 major incidents during November, all affecting on line service
provision. Three major incidents were attributed to the ADSL service infrastructure and the other major incident affected core network
connectivity for a period of 4 minutes affecting all on line network traffic. All these incidents have been fully investigated and corrective
preventative actions put in place.

OBC performance continued a positive performance with all OLA targets achieved. In addition, the rollout status of ADSL connected
branches has increased to over 10,600 branches, and the Postshop rollout has progressed to 165 branches.

Of particular note is the marked increase in the volume of incidents which have been logged with the Horizon Service Desk (an increase
from an average of 13,500 incidents per month to just under 17,000). This is attributed to two main reasons: 1) a seasonal increase in
hardware incident volumes which is in line with the previous year increase 2) an increase in system or software related incidents as a
result of Branch Trading introduction. Despite these considerable increases, all service desk service levels have been achieved.

2.1.2 Availability and Problem Management

Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for November 2005 there were 4 Major Incidents.
There were a further 2 service affecting incidents within the Fujitsu domain and 2 incidents outside the Fujitsu domain.

Major Incidents:

Monday 7th November.

Major Incident: An incident on the BAS Router GB_TELHS1-R1-003 occurred at 09:25. There were 325 branches connected via this BAS
Router and at the time of this failure it caused 241 branches (74%) to lose their on-line services. Between 09:40 and 09:52 all branches
were without On-Line Services whilst C&W (Cable & Wireless) reloaded the router. All On-Line Services were restored by 10:45. A Major
Incident report was produced under call number: E-0511070282. Problem Management calls PM0000688 and P10001628 refer to this
incident.

Monday 14th November.

Major Incident: An incident occurred on the Network between 10:43 and 10:47 which affected several circuits including the IP Select
circuits to EPAY (Kelting House & Southfields) and the circuits to the A&L (Carlton Park & Bootle). These circuits were only affected for the
four minute period. The incident also affected a total of 244 ADSL and ISDN branches connecting via Watford & Kearsley routers, resulting
in a loss of On-Line Services. Calls to the HSD increased due to the loss of On-Line Services from these branches at 10:55, but had
subsided by 11:27. The fault was due to a Cable & Wireless engineer applying an access control change to routers within the core C&W
network. This change was considered by C&W to be very low risk, and so it was internally authorized, without prior POA approval.
Unfortunately the engineer made a human error whilst applying the change and accidentally configured a router incorrectly. This was the
cause of the failure and was quickly corrected by the engineer restricting the failure to the four minute outage. All On-Line Services were
restored to branches by 11:22. A Major Incident report was produced reference E-0511140442 and there were 3 other calls associated
with this incident; E-0511140479, E-0511140546 and E-0511140564. The Problem Management calls are PM0000690 & P10001638 have
been updated and agreement with POL PM to close the incident.

Tuesday 15th November.

Major Incident: Several ADSL branches connecting via Watford BAS Router GB_WAHP03-R10-007 displayed intermittent transaction
timeouts across all On-Line services. The incident started at 13:33 and continued until 17:12. Initial investigations pointed to Broadband
work being undertaken by BT at this time, but was then discounted as BT were adamant that the errors they were seeing were from the
C&W domain. C&W took some corrective actions overnight to diagnose/eliminate possible failing components. The 2 co-axial cables
connecting the ATM to the Multiplexor were replaced, after which the ATM STM1 card P3.3.1 was replaced. C&W has continued to monitor
and to date no more failures have been identified. A Major Incident report has been produced, call number E-0510240983 refers. Problem
Management calls PM0000689 and P10001632 refer and are on monitor. The cables tested ok, so STM1 card has been sent back to Alcatel
for fault evaluation, time scale for a response is 60 days.

Tuesday 29th November

Major Incident: There was an incident affecting All On-Line services to 910 outlets, connected to BAS Router GB_KEARO2-R10-001.

At 12.10 hrs, C&W advised that there was a memory leakage on the affected BAS router that had become critical. Through a cross domain
conference call it was agreed that the safest activity would be to reload the affected router. The reload took place at 12.30 hrs, and by
12.45 approximately 30% of the affected outlets had returned to normal service. Normal service was restored to all outlets by 13:08. Calls
E-0511290577 and E-0511290779 refer.

Other incidents in Fujitsu Services domain:

Tuesday 8th November.

The Start of Day tests indicated that Watford BAS router 003 had 5.2% (15 branches) of its connected branches without On-Line Services.
This was due to overnight weather conditions and reboots at the start of the next business day restored service. E-0511071129 refers.

In addition the “Ping” scripts indicated a 29 of the VSAT branches without connectivity. Investigations showed that although some had
been affected by overnight weather conditions and came back in at start of day, several were closed and only 3 branches required a call
logged.

Wednesday 16th November.

An overnight failure to the BT links feeding the VSAT router at Docklands (Planned outage by BT but not communicated to C&W) occurred
from 22:00 till 03:00 meant that the normal overnight Reference data drop to the VSAT counters at 02:00 was missed and this download
took place at 07:00 on the next available window. This had an affect to the VSAT bandwidth available at the start of the business day
resulting in a loss of On-Line Services to 38 Branches. Call E-0511170545 refers. The availability of the bandwidth increased during the
day with service returning to branches. Intervention by the SSC was required to clear out stale messages from the BT outage to ensure all
the bandwidth was available to the VSAT network. Full service was restored 18:53. Call E-0511160910 refers.

There were no VIP sites affected this month.

Incidents Outside the Fujitsu Domain:

Friday 25th November.

An incident between Link and the Co-Op bank between 13:00 and 13:36 caused 1322 transactions to be declined. The service returned to
normal at 13:36.

Monday 28th November.

A re-occurrence of the Co-Op banking incident between 13:00 and 13:40 resulted in a total loss of service from Link to Co-op & Smile
banking system. There 1571 transactions declined to the Co-op bank, and 246 transactions to the Smile banking system. Normal service
was resumed at 13:40.

2.1.3 OBC Branch Change Service Summary

November 2005 was a very productive month and showing that the failure rate was the best result for the reporting year, a very
successful month. A total of 277 OBC changes were received during the month of November, with 20 changes being cancelled. A total of
257 change requests were completed, with Fujitsu being responsible for 11 change requests not being completed on the target date. For
Fujitsu this represents a failure rate of 4.28% against the 5% target rate. As there were 2 line plant failures this represents a failure rate
of 3.50% against the 4% target. This is a difference, compared to the October calculations of 1.32% against the 5% target rate and
0.6% against the 4% target rate. Post Office were responsible for 4 change requests not being completed on the target date, which
represents 1.56% against the 4% target, which is a difference against the October calculations of 2.92%.

Fujitsu’s direct failures comprised of 2 process, 1 hardware, 4 software and 4 communications.

Post Office Limited direct failures comprised of 3 process and 1 access.

Corrective Actions

Investigations continue into the compression of message store data to speed up file transfer over ISDN communications. Fujitsu has
however established that configuration times can be decreased on “Relocations” by ensuring slaves are in “Riposte” position before
Gateway configuration takes place. This is communicated to all Fujitsu Engineers at the time of attending a Relocation change.

Recently two failures have been as a direct result of ‘unfamiliar’ OBC Engineers attending changes. Assurances have been given by Fujitsu
Engineering that these were isolated incidentsss&ohmgimeering are currently investigating the viability of a dedicated OBC engineering
resource. These assessments are expected to conclude early in the New Year. Potential benefits are that all OBC work would always be
undertaken by the same engineers and thus familiarity would be commonplace.

2.1.4 ADSL Migration

We are continuing with the migration of ISDN to ADSL and at the end of October 10392 branches were using ADSL comms.
The migration has achieved a total of 625 outlets migrated this month.

The Rollout programme is now nearing the end with a total of 802 left to migrate. Along side this Fujitsu will undertake a programme to
remigrate all branches that had reverted or opened onto to ISDN comms as part of an Operational Business Change.

2.1.5 Icon Service

Icon Batch 2005_02 has been put on one of the RDDT validation counters for checking.
This batch is due to be released with S90 Counter code.

2.1.6 Business Continuity

During November the Business Continuity Team successfully completed an operational test for the Generic Agent server and an
operational retest of the KMS server.

Operational re-testing of the Network Banking Client interfaces and the data-centre catalyst switch was also performed. This
testing identified new network incidents which are currently being investigated.

Business Continuity Procedural walk-throughs were successfully conducted for the Database Server, System Management Centre and the
AP Client and EDG gateways.

Work is progressing in publishing revised business continuity plans which include new S90 and S92 infrastructure and services.

27 Message Broadcasts were processed in November.

3.2.2 ONLINE NETWORK SERVICES

During November the HSD received a total of 1865 reported incidents with 1243 related to ADSL network faults. The major incidents on
the 07", 15th and 29th were responsible for the main increase above the October volumes. Fujitsu have continued to apply pressure on
C&W and they have now tightened their change process to reduce the number of changes that are applied during trading hours.
Additional scripting has now been deployed to reduce the risk of human error while applying standard changes to BAS routers.

The sudden frost and cold conditions caused an increased branch incident volume affecting the PSTN lines and ISDN lines. The MTFR
suffered as BT field engineering were insufficiently resourced to service all these calls. BT’s seasonal engineering problem continues to
cause an irregular service characteristic across the year. The matter has again been raised through carrier relations for the attention of BT
Wholesale. In addition we have asked C&W to instigate a service improvement plan for branch fault resolution time on ISDN and VSAT.

An ADSL enhanced care trial began at 1000 large branches on the 1st and will run for two calendar months. At the end of the two months
the data will be analysed to measure the percentage of actual enhanced care incidents achieving the SLA and the improvement in MTBF
achieved at these branches.

Online transaction call volumes also decreased to 199 with most actual faults relating to the incidents within the Link and Co-Op domain.
Enhanced Care Update

During November there were 3 actual faults at the 1000 branches covered by Enhanced Care. Only 1 of these faults contributed to BT-
Broadband's SLA criteria and we had a 100% success rate. The service has cost £9,719/month and the December metrics will be reported
next month. The service is scheduled to revert back to Standard Care on 31/12/05.

The new RAG status for the key cross domain problems are based on the criteria —
Business Impact BI score Root Cause RC score

High (H) >20 outlets affected 3 Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place 3

Medium (M) >1 <20 outlets affected 2 Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed 2
or workaround in place

Low (L) <1 outlet affected 1 Fix implemented and problem being monitored 1

RAG status = BI score x RC score = (RED = 6-9) (AMBER = 3-6) (GREEN = 1-2)

The differentiator to be applied between a RED & AMBER RAG status score of 6 depends on whether the RC is known and whether a
workaround is in place i.e. if a the score of 6 is derived by a “Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place = RC score of
3” then the RAG will be red.

If the RC score was derived by “Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed or a workaround in place = RC score
of 2 then the RAG will be amber.

7.11 POSTSHOPS

— ————=_=_—=_—=_=—=—==

There have been 5 instances throughout November when the file delivery has been late. In the first 9 weeks of polling (from Aug1) we
had no issues. However, as the number of sites increased, the frequency on late polls increased.

A number of actions have been identified to resolve this problem:

Implementation of parallel polling to cut down the session time. We have currently moved from 1 to 2 polling machines with a split of
about 80 /70 and by Christmas we will move to 4 polling machines with about 40 sites on each machine which will take us into a safe zone
for session lengths. The effects of this change will only just be cutting into the performance.

There will be other issues that cause late polls and has each one occurs we will investigate and put a corrective action in place. An
example of this is the late polls of the last couple of days:-

On the 21% Nov we did not late poll but 57 sites failed to respond to polling (we expect an average of 4 or 5)

On the 22" there was a late poll as one of the polling machines stopped at one particular branch and kept retrying

On the 23 the same machine again stopped as at the same Branch (this was down to a corrupt data base file, now corrected).
Every new problem will be treated in the same way — determining the cause and correcting the issue
