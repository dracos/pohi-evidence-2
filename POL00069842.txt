Dear Mike and Geoff
I refer to my telephone conversation with Mike on 1 November.
I attach the following, for your information:

1. Witness statement of Paul Williamson and exhibit. Paul is a fraud prevention and
investigation manager at Alliance & Leicester (formerly Girobank). His stated that when
deposits of cash are made, the sub postmaster has a deposit slip. At the end of each day, the
sub postmaster produces a report from Horizon called the Horizon Daily Record of Deposits and
checks that the transaction details shown on the physical deposit slips match the information on
the HDR. He then sends the deposit slips accompanying HDR to the Alliance & Leicester
processing centre. They are processed together. If the two do not match, then an error notice
will be generated.

You will already have seen from the witness statements of Ken Crawley, Michael Johnson,
Gillian Hoyland and Wendy Smith that there are similar processes for pensions, lottery, cheques
and APS transactions, The P.O's point is therefore that if the computer did not capture all the
information (which is denied), this would have been picked up when the paperwork Mr
Castleton sent each day to the relevant processing centre did not match what he recorded into
his computer. This is a fundamental part of the Post Office's case.

2. Copies of three spreadsheets listing (a) notes (b) coin and (c) notes and coin totalled which
the Post Office's records show were delivered to Marine Drive branch from 18 July 2003 to 25
March 2004.
