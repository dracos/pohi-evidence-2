AUTOMATION OF POST OFFICES AND BENEFIT PAYMENTS

I have now seen your letter of 17 October to Alistair Darling and his response to you of
28 October about the need to discuss questions affecting the future of the Payment
Card programme and the post office network in the round.

You are aware of the repeated delays which have beset this large and important IT
programme which is jointly sponsored by DSS and Post Office Counters Limited (POCL).
It is a massive challenge, particularly for POCL. They, for the first time, must manage
and control the development, installation and support of a sophisticated IT system
across their entire network. This would be a daunting task even for an organisation with
previous experience of this scale of IT programme.

My officials continue to give the highest possible priority to working with POCL and ICL
Pathway towards identifying an acceptable plan to successfully implement the Card
Payment programme. There is, however, no guarantee that the programme can succeed
on its original terms. Jt is therefore only prudent to begin examining alternative means
of benefit delivery that would also support our wider objectives of reducing fraud and
providing an efficient and cost effective means of paying benefits

I should be in a position to fully understand all my options and to recommend a way
forward after the Christmas recess. Delaying a decision beyond that timescale will
inevitably bring added risks to my Department’s work programme. It would, for
example, complicate the contractual position with ICL Pathway; involve further
significant spending which might ultimately be nugatory; and it would continue to hold
up other DSS IT work programmes which are essential to our aims of securing a
modern welfare delivery system.

1 do, however, share your growing concern about the future viability of POCL. I also
support the approach which Alistair has suggested although I would be concerned if we
became tied to a CSR timetable which delayed, unnecessarily, the decisions which need
to be taken. I would therefore propose that my officials contact your's straightaway so
that we can start to build a common understanding of all the issues.
