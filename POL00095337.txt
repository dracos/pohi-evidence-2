QUESTIONING

Commence questioning interviewee. The following should be noted:

Ensure that everyone present speaks clearly and that the suspect clarifies any nods, shrugs or gestures, i.e. “You
have just nodded. Would you please answer the question verbally”

All items shown to the interviewee should be described concisely and given item numbers

Be careful not to say anything that could be interpreted as oppression or construed as inducement
