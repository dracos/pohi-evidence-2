RE: FUTURE OF THE POST OFFICE BEYOND 2021

Thank you for your letter dated 4 December 2019 informing me of Post Office Limited’s
progress towards commercial sustainability.

lam grateful to POL’s leadership, staff and the thousands of postmasters for their tireless
work in delivering both a stable network of post offices and profitability ahead of the original
Three-Year Plan. I also welcome your confirmation that POL will not require further
investment funding beyond 2021. Taken together this represents a significant shared
achievement.

Looking ahead, the Department will expect POL to maintain this positive trajectory, while
continuing to improve its relationship with postmasters.

I look forward to discussing your plans for the future of the Post Office in the coming
months. In light of the recent settlement, I also remain keen to hear about the
implementation of further improvements in the culture of the Company, including training
and support for postmasters and any consequential action required from the litigation. In
addition, I have said to Tim that I would find it helpful to receive a presentation from you
about the results of your Purpose, Strategy and Growth work at some point early this year.

Alex Chisholm
