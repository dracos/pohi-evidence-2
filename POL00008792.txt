POST OFFICE LTD -v-

Thank you for your minute of 4 January 2011. In my opinion the evidence is sufficient to
afford a realistic prospect of conviction of the above named on the charge set out on the
attached Schedule. My understanding of the evidence is that the Defendant was using money
in the post office and his stock-takings to pay bills and purchase stock. The money ran out.
Cash held at the office was the Defendants property. He was unable to obtain any loans and
accordingly he had to find the money from somewhere. He then purported to place cash
into the post office supported by documentation which was used to inflate his Alliance &
Leicester account. He then transferred money from the Alliance & Leicester account to his
HSBC account which was used to pay monies owed to Post Office Ltd and no doubt to obtain
cash to enable him to continue to run his business. As his business was not making a profit
the amount required was inflated over a period of time.

I am not convinced therefore that there is any charge of theft that can easily be preferred in
this case and accordingly I have charged the allegation of fraud. I am also not convinced that
money-laundering is an appropriate way to address this issue.

If I have misunderstood this case, please contact me immediately.

In view of the serious nature of the alleged offence involving a loss of over £95.000 to Post
Office Ltd, this case should be prosecuted.

This case is, in my opinion, more suitable for trial in the Crown Court.

In the event that the prosecution is authorised, please obtain summonses.
