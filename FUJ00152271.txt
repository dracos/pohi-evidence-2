1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for April 07 there was 1 Major Incident, five service affecting
incidents within the Fujitsu domain, 1 incident relating to VIP sites and two further service affecting incidents
outside the Fujitsu domain. Details are given in the Incident Overview below.

The major incident occurred on 6" April: - a T40 incremental software drop in preparation for the branch router
caused issues with the ISDN network, affecting up to 1085 connected branches. A workaround was put in place
and the issue was resolved before start of trading on the next working day.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.7% and 99.2% respectively. Daily tracker
performance remains good, and the weekly dashboard is proving valuable in jointly identifying areas of concern,
in particular around the management of intermittent faults. 89 OBC changes were delivered in the month and all
the OBC targets were exceeded with all OLAs met.

Incoming calls to the desk reduced this month, but network calls showed an increase, in direct relation to the
various incidents during this period. However, resolution times have remained within expectation. Almost 1 in 4
counters now has a new Epson Counter Printer, and the migration to IPStream is nearing completion.

Despite continued focus, the Engineering SLTs have not recovered to meet target. The recovery plan continues
to identify areas for improvement and the effect on branch trading and user feedback is being closely monitored
to ensure that the impact on the business is fully appreciated whilst the recovery is progressed

A total of 85 complaints were received, 47 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, the increase being
largely attributable to online service problems arising from network migration activity. 3 were for investigation
and comment: - of these, none were agreed to be justified. A further review of the complaints process has been
undertaken to ensure that the level of feedback and appropriate action is taken in every case. The revised
process is under discussion and should be implemented shortly.

Major Incidents: Friday 6 April. T40 increment s/w drop in preparation for the branch router
caused issues with the ISDN network, affecting up to 1085 connected
branches through the LNS routers. This was due to static configured return
routes through the LNS routers. This required corrective actions by the
Network team, TDA and C&W to produce dynamic routes through just 2
remaining LNS routers 1 & 61. Issue resolved by Monday AM. Call E-
0704060052 and MI report refers.

Other incidents in Fujitsu Monday 2nd April. IP Stream router “Hardy” blipped for 2 minutes affecting

investigation shows a memory upgrade required to allow future diagnostics
and corrective actions to take place before reset of router occurs. Call E-
0704021070 refers.

Friday 13th April. Timeouts experienced on the A&L link to Wigan.
Timeouts experienced between 10:00 and 11:30, retries working
successfully. Problem identified to WAN circuit within C&W from Wigan to
Leicester. C&W with BT are currently investigating and monitoring the link for
further failures. Call E-0704130308 refers.

Friday 13th April. A loss of on-line services, to 42 VSAT connected
branches, from 11:30 to 12:00. The fault was identified to a failure within the
BT switch at the Turin base station. Call E-0704130372 refers.

affecting up to 238 branches. Upon investigation found to be an incident

Manchester. Call E-0704250762 refers.

had dropped off line the pervious evening. A re-boot of the server was
required to restore service. Investigations found human error in missing alert
overnight, process re-emphasized to all shift members. Call E-0704260331
refers.

Commentary
The number of complaints logged against Fujitsu Services in April has increased, however of those passed for
formal investigation, none were classified as justified.

Engineering /Printing Problems.

The Engineering Service Improvement Plan weekly conference calls continue to review A priority faults, and a
meeting has been scheduled to review the end-to-end process to remove any points of delay. This meeting is
scheduled for early June.

The Epson printer rollout programme to replace the Ithaca printers is underway and progress is continuing
according to schedule. To date 7621 new printers have been installed, which equates to 22% of the printers in
the estate. As this project continues the call, visit volumes and subsequently complaints volumes are all
forecasted to drop. The reduction in the number of counter printer faults and engineering visits will have a
knock on benefit on scheduling engineering visits. As part of the project, detailed analysis of the calls has been
undertaken and an issue with printers printing blank receipts has been identified. This is the subject of Problem
Management activity and discussions are underway with Epson, the technical designers and the service desk to
provide workaround and preventative maintenance instructions to address this issue, whilst investigating and
removing root cause.

Online Services

The number of complaints for Online services has increased in April, which is primarily due to the
implementation of phase II of the migration to IP stream, which has seen an increase of 30% in the number of
online services faults logged with the Service Desk. As a consequence the number of intermittent faults and
branches with BNR installed was at its highest for a year. The migration has now completed, and we would
expect to see the call volumes and number of complaints return to normal volumes. GSMs have been deployed
in branches with migration issues to ensure service continuity while the issue with the primary comms is
resolved by BT.

Branches experiencing solid down faults are still running at extremely low levels, with on average 4 branches a
day experiencing solid down faults, and on several occasions during April, there were no branches with solid
down faults.

Work continues with BT to improve the resolution process for faults relating to the physical phone line and faults
internal to the branch.

Sense & Respond / Service Improvement Activities

The progress on the current Sense and Respond projects as detailed below:

° Network Issues:

focus has been made to begin tracking ADSL and ISDN call volumes and the mean

time to clear. This data will be captured each month and trend analysis will be conducted to highlight
areas of required focus. Future projects include restructuring the Communications Management Team
to enhance call ownership across the team to ensure calls are followed up and closed more efficiently
within the SLA period. Also, renaming the communication stacks will be a focus point to ensure more

effective tracking of calls in the future.

e PinPads: Sense and Respond investigation has identified that software changes could reduce
spurious Pin Pad errors and this will subject to continued investigation. There has been a need
identified to amend Powerhelp Pin Pad coding to enable more effective tracking of calls and a key

future project has been identified to pilot Pin Pad card reader cleaning kits.

e Counter Printer Data Issues: The Epson printer roll out programme is presently a third of the way in
progress and the call rate for Epson printers higher than expected. Total Epson calls received in April
was 501, of which 84% of calls were resolved by HSD. A key future project will be looking at

developing a PM hints/tips document to help reduce printer call volumes.

e Chasing call failure demand: The objective is to reduce the number of engineering calls made to
engineering. Key actions to achieve the project focus include enhancing PM education/awareness
about SLAs and to provide advice regarding not calling for ETAs on C priority calls until the SLA has

passed.

« Software errors: A new project has been initiated which is looking at Software issues and identifying

the route cause of these issues. The initial problem type being investigated is software freezes

e Engineer Calls. An additional objective regarding engineering calls is to reduce the amount of calls to
HSD from Touch, Fujitsu and ROMEC engineers. This is to help increase agent availability for taking
PM calls. Calls logged for engineering have been looked at and split between HSD and CMT. The

different types of enquires have been identified; the main 2 types being ‘Advice’ and ‘LAN’.

A new Sense and Respond champion for the desk has been appointed and will continue to progress the
projects. A full Sense & Respond report is produced by the Sense & Respond champion on a monthly basis,

and this is distributed separately to the SRB.

5.2 Engineering Service

April 07 has seen the engineering call volumes fall to under 6000 calls with an increase in Epson Counter
printer calls and notable decreases in numbers of Ithaca counter printer and screen calls. Compared to April
last year, calls for the OKI printer have increased significantly.

The Epson counter printer rollout has so far seen 7621 new printers installed into 1308 branches. Compared to
the previous month, April has seen a decrease in A priority SLT conformance and a slight increase in C priority
SLT conformance. The A priority within 4 hour fix decreased by 3.6% to 73.0% and the C priority by 18:30 next
day calls increased by 0.3% to 93.2%.

There were notable impacts on the 4hr engineering service from the IP Stream project, with many more
complex intermittent calls, OTI downtime and a higher than expected volume of DOA’s on the A & B Base units.
IP Stream issues have been raised with BT. In week three, the OTI hardware was replaced and since then no
OTI downtime has been recorded. The volume of DOA’s is under investigation but may be attributed to an
increase in network call build failures.

Base unit builds remain the single highest point of failure for A priority conformance. Many of the Epson Counter
printer calls can be prevented through the education of branch staff. Communications have been and will
continue to be sent out. The Service Improvement Plan remains in place and is reviewed weekly to address
issues affecting SLA conformance.

April has again seen all counter availability targets met with all conformances in excess of 99%.

5.3. Branch Network Services / Central Network

Branch Network Services
During February there were 1,654 calls logged across all Network Types into the HSD.

SLT's

The ‘Priority A - 6 hours’ SLT saw 47 failures from a total of 1229 closed calls measured, resulting in 96.2%
achievement. A contributing factor to the SLT conformance miss can be attributed to a number of issues
resulting from IP Migration, in addition to a higher then normal call raised rate. These issues have been flagged
to BT via Service Implementation and Branch Network Services and actions are being undertaken to resolve.
Post Office are being engaged where necessary in an additional attempt to resolve the longer running faults.

There is no longer a C priority measure as all Network related calls are prioritised as “A” priority.

Other issues in April
After extensive investigation we have identified a number of issues within the BT Infrastructure domain. We are

currently working closely with all Internal SDU’s and BT directly to resolve. BT also reported a MSO (major
service outage) within the Liverpool area, which impacted a small number of branches resulting in “Intermittent”
communications. BT has resolved this issue and the affected branches have resumed communications.

CMT have now completed the move to “ownership” of a single incident, which means that each member of the
Comms Management Team has responsibility for owning an incident from the point of inception to resolution. It
is expected that this move will increase the individual focus of each incident and therefore should result in
higher SLT conformance results.

Central Network

The T40 increment s/w drop major incident resulted in 293 calls to the HSD over the Easter bank holiday
weekend from ISDN branches that were unable to build a connection to the datacentres. The transactions
attempted at these branches during this incident also impacted on the overall monthly Fixed (99%) and Dialled
(98%) connection reliability SLAs. These branches also incurred very high call charges (absorbed within Fujitsu)
due to the numerous reconnection attempts.

The IP Stream faults resulted in 96 HSD calls. The VSAT fault resulted in 0 HSD calls. The A&L and LiNK faults
resulted in 41 HSD calls.

5.5 Data Centre Operations Service / Data File Transfer

There was one major incident which occurred on Friday 6" of April in relation to the Data Centre Operations
Service within the Data Centre central Infrastructure. Please see section 2.0.

Throughout the month there was a total of 166 operational changes, 153 made by Post Office Account and 13
by Post Office Limited 3” parties, there were no service impacts as a result of those changes being
implemented.

Currently there are 19 customer facing risks identified in the Data Centre Operations service classified by RAG
status as 1 red, 5 amber and 13 green risks. The red risk relates to the limit on the number of services which
can be supported by Escher Design studio/Mails. Post Office Limited wish to add further new services and it is
considered that this will exceed the current limit.

5.5.1. Day J Failures

DFD Outbound figures were impacted by a total of 2 No Lets this month, which is a increase on the previous
couple of months. This resulted in 2 Day J targets failing, however the APS Outbound Day J target passed as
there were no APS transactions from any of these 2 Branches during the down periods detailed below.

The BAU and escalation processes have been reiterated to the team that manage the non-polling calls.

123845 — 12 days late —- Problem resolved, site now health checking.

A call was logged for an intermittent comms issue on 6" April (last EOD received was on 12” April). BT
investigated the fault and it was finally resolved on 27" April. A data retrieval call was raised on 20" April due to
the length of time BT were taking to investigate the issue, however due to a shortage of PSTN modems in this
area, DR was not completed by the target date.

169828 — 15 days late —- Problem resolved, site now health checking.

A call was logged for a comms issue on 17" April and passed to BT for investigation of the satellite system. A
data retrieval call was logged on 20" April, but was not completed within timescales because the first engineer
did not have a Day J laptop, and the second engineer had a faulty Day J laptop. The fault was finally resolved
on 30" April by the FMS engineer attending to do DR.
