To Counsel to the Inquiry for the attention of the Chair, Sir Wyn Williams

Note to the Inquiry re Sir Alex Chisholm and constructive knowledge
of the Swift Review by BIS, BEIS & DBT

1. It is submitted that Sir Alex. Chisholm’s department, BEIS, were on
notice that the SWIFT Review was completed, and ought to have
followed up on it. It was originally the brainchild of Baroness Neville-
Rolfe (“BNR”), and the predecessor department was keeping a
watching brief on it, so it should not have been allowed to
disappear.

2. On 10 September 2015, Baroness Neville-Rolfe wrote to Tim
Parker to express the Government's concern at the ongoing,
unresolved Horizon IT issue, and to ask him what action he
intended to take.' He replied on 30 September, saying that he
intended to appoint a QC to advise him.? Baroness Neville-Rolfe is
quite clear that that idea had come from her — but (perhaps)
optically it had to appear as if it was the new Chair’s decision.

3. A briefing note from November 2015 for the Secretary of State, the
Rt Hon Sajid Javid, supports the above thesis. It stated:

Tim Parker agreed with Baroness Neville-Rolfe that he would
undertake a review into the Post Office "Horizon" IT system
and various claims that subpostmasters have been wrongly
prosecuted as a result of faults in the system. Tim has
appointed a QC to assist him in that review, and expects to
report back to Baroness Neville-Rolfe early in the New Year.
