Dear Rob,

Thanks for your assistance this morning. A short note to confirm our earlier conversation about
the outcome of the hearing:

Coram: HHJ Forrester
Contra: Miss Mottram
Also present: Phil Bradley, Birchall Blackburn solicitors

Order:

1) Experts to meet and produce a joint report on Horizon, including a schedule of agreement and
disagreement, by 29" October 2010.

2) Relist for mention and fix on 10" November 2010.

3) Within 7 days the prosecution shall disclose the following, or explain why the same should not
be disclosed:
a) Vouchers
b) Business Support Centre Records
c) Electronic Funds Transfer Records

In relation to 1) above, this order stemmed from your Instructions to me that this approach has
been followed in the case of Misra. It seems like the most sensible way to answer the issue as to
the reliability of Horizon. We have discussed the somewhat unfocussed nature of Mr.
McLachlan’s interim report and other concerns arising therefrom. In line with your Instructions,
1 indicated that the defence expert could have supervised access to Horizon during any meeting.

In relation to 2) above, I had submitted that the return mention and fix should be listed on the
29th October, as I am aware that Andy Rutter is scheduled to enter a 30 day trial the following
week. However, HHJ Forrester would not have been available on that date and wanted to keep a
handle on the case.

In relation to 3) above, the defence state that they have had material of this kind disclosed in
similar cases.

The Court are anxious to list a trial date. I have pointed out that the defendant has accepted an
accounting imbalance of approximately £26,000 and further accepted falsifying records to
conceal the discrepancy. The only issue is whether this was done honestly to cover up the
technical failings of Horizon, or dishonestly to cover up her own theft. The experts’ report may
significantly narrow the trial issues.
