1. This is the skeleton argument of the Claimant (“C”) on the Pre-trial Review (“PTR”) in
preparation for the 10 day trial fixed on 22" June 2006 and listed to start on 4" December
2006. A short procedural chronology is attached to this document.

2. In the action C claims £25,858.95 being monies reported as received by the Defendant
(“D”) as agent for C but for which he has failed to account. D’s unparticularised defence
is apparently that the losses are not real but are occasioned by problems with C’s
computerized accounting system. Since D alleges there were no “real” losses, he says his
subsequent dismissal by C as a sub-postmaster was wrongful and (by recent amendment
for which no permission has been sought or obtained) he claims damages of £11,250
(reduced from £250,000).

3. This matter was last before the Court (Master Turner) for a Case Management Conference
on 23" October 2006. The Master gave directions for pleadings consequential on
clarificatory amendments to C’s statements of case and set a timetable for exchange of
witness statements and sequential exchange of expert evidence (given that it was D that
was seeking to suggest that his experts were going to identify the issues said by D to give
rise to his reported losses and the date for exchange of reports had long since past). D
was to serve his expert evidence by 4 pm on 10" November 2006 and exchange of

witness statements was to take place by the same time.

Since that hearing, although a draft Amended Defence that contained both consequential
and new amendments was served, D has failed to verify that with a signed statement of
truth. D’s unverified Amended Defence alleges for the first time that the signed Cash
Account (Final) for week 51 was not signed by him, although the document in C’s

disclosure bears a signature in his name and apparently in his handwriting.

Further, although C offered to exchange witness statements on 10 November 2006, D

has not been ready to exchange, nor has he served any expert evidence.

On Friday 17" November 2006 C sent by DX signed witness statements from its 15
witnesses of fact to D’s then solicitors to hold to C’s order until they were in a position to

exchange.

On Monday 20" November 2006 D served a notice of change and is now acting in person.

He has still not offered any statements for exchange.

C has 15 witnesses of fact, all of whom are the subject of witness summonses requiring
attendance on the first day of the trial. C also has an accounting expert warned for
attendance at the trial in the expectation that D was going to serve expert evidence by 10"
November 2006 to which they might be required to reply by 24" November 2006. No
reports in answer have been prepared as D has not served any expert evidence to answer

pursuant to the Order of Master Turner of 23" October 2006.

C sent D a draft index for the proposed bundles for trial and invited a response by 22
November 2006. No response has been received. Accordingly bundles are being

prepared on the basis of the draft index sent to D.

C continues to prepare for trial starting on 4" December 2006 but is entitled to know what
case it is to meet and the evidence that is to be relied upon by D. This is particularly so in
circumstances where D has made serious but unparticularised allegations about C’s
business systems and the costs of defending those allegations is so great in comparison to

the amounts at issue.
