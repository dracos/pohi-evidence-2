Dear All,

Please see below a readout of Kelly's meeting with Nick Read, CEO for Post Office Limited
(POL) on Wednesday 224 January 2020.

Attendees:

Kelly Tolhurst MP

Nick Read, CEO for POL
Jessica Tysoe, Private Secretary

-START-

Introductions
¢ Kelly started the meeting by making Nick aware that one of the newly elected MP’s had

been talking to Radio 4 about the British Gas/Payzone issue on the morning of the 220.
She said that she was disappointed that the newly elected MP’s had been going straight
to the media having not discussed the issue with the Minister responsible first. She has

passed this back to the Whips to ensure it doesn’t continue to happen. Nick appreciated
that Kelly was keen to defend the Post Office to her parliamentary colleagues, he agreed

that Kelly could have assured them that it is the fault of British Gases and not caused by
POL.

Litigation

¢ Kelly thanked Nick for his hard work on reaching the recent litigation mediation
settlement. She said she was pleased that it was reached within the amount agreed.
Nick was grateful and explained how he had never had to be part of a mediation quite
like it.

Kelly mentioned that she has received a large amount of correspondence since the
settlement asking whether government will be paying out the £57.75m, Nick noted this.
She also said that she will continue to push back on MP's where appropriate.

Kelly expressed her concern about the upcoming STARLING litigation case which is due
to be heard in October this year. She asked that Nick uses the Labour Markets team
within BEIS for policy expertise as it will be an invaluable resource to them. They both
noted that they could not seek legal advice from the policy team.

Nick raised some concerns about the CWU’s representation in the case, Kelly asked to
continue to be kept up to date (Action - Policy Team). She also asked that her meeting
with the CWU is rescheduled as it was pulled due to the election (Action - Tolhurst
PO).

Culture Change, Purpose and Five-Year Plan

¢ Nick updated Kelly that the work he commissioned from McKinsey is due by the end of
the month and that he will be taking a draft of the Five-Year Plan to the January POL
Board meeting (and a further iteration in February) with the aim of Kelly having the
opportunity to feed into it ahead of it being agreed at the Board meeting on 24th March.
Kelly responded by saying that she was meeting with officials shortly to discuss it and
wants to meet with him again once he has some concrete plans as a result of
McKinsey’s findings. (Action — Officials to advise on a date)
Nick emphasised that he is particularly focusing on workplace culture and the
relationship between POL and postmasters. He said these issues were central to the
litigation and whilst it has been settled there is still a long way to go to reset the
relationship. Kelly agreed with his approach.
Nick talked Kelly though his recent changes to the senior leadership team including a
new HR Director and Communications Lead. Kelly was pleased that he had brought in
someone external that he trusted and that the other was internal, showcasing the
opportunity to progress within the organisation.
Kelly said that she was keen to get the work on the Long-Term Vision and Five-Year
Plan locked down quickly and that they should be working together so that they
complement each other. Nick agreed and they both agreed that they should aim to do
this in time for budget/spending review.

Spending Review/Subsidy
e Kelly reminded Nick that POL will need to provide us with timely and robust evidence,
but said she was grateful for the material they have provided already. Nick agreed POL
will continue to do this.

POca
¢ They briefly touched on POca and Kelly said that she had run into Guy Opperman MP,
who asked to meet with Kelly. Kelly said she wants this meeting scheduled ASAP as
Guy coming directly to her is a change of direction since their correspondence last year
(Action - Tolhurst PO).
