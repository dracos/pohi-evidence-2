Dear Sirs
Post Office Limited v Mrs T Somaskandarajah
Thank you for your letter dated 29 April.

We have the following comments in response:

1 You seem possibly to be under a misapprehension that Mrs Somaskandarajah has only been
suspended from office rather than dismissed. Mrs Somaskandarajah was suspended on or around
23 October 2004. Her contract was then summarily terminated on or around 21 November 2005.
She subsequently appealed against the decision to terminate her contract but the decision was
upheld by the Appeals Manager.

Documents

2 You state in your 23 August 2007 letter that the documents that are missing including daily cash
declarations, AP Reports, P and A Reports, green Giro reports, trial cash reports and final cash
accounts. However, on 7 June 2007, we sent to you various documentation which included copies
of the former Sub Post Mistress Statement of Errors together with the underlying documentation
for every error mentioned on the Statement of Outstanding debt. This includes, for example,
Girobank slips and records of deposits that you subsequently requested on 23 August 2007. We
also sent you the final cash account, balance snapshots dated 21 October 2004 and photocopies
of two slips of paper as long ago as 17 August 2007.

3 You initially stated that documents were removed by Mr Steve George and Ms Kerry James. After
we told you that there was no lady called Kerry James but a man called Ceri James who denied
removing any documents, you then stated that a red ring binder was removed from the branch by
Mr George. However, Mr George does not recall removing a red ring binder.

4 We have already supplied you with the relevant documents (and various others you requested).
As long ago as 21 June 2007, we asked you to explain how each of the remaining documents was
relevant to the Claim and exactly how they are going to help resolve it. You have never answered
that question. Instead, you simply stated that the documents belong to your client. We disagree.
The relationship between our respective clients was one of principal and agent. Accordingly, any
financial documents produced by Mrs Somaskandarajah were held in her capacity as agent for
Post Office Limited (POL). Furthermore, pursuant specifically to Clause 4 of Section 12 of the
Contract, Mrs Somaskandarajah was obliged to produce such accounts immediately whenever
requested by a person duly authorised by POL. We therefore do not accept that you are entitled
to the documents. Furthermore, you have consistently failed to explain their relevance.

3 month’s income

8 Even if POL had no grounds upon which to have terminated the contract (which is denied), the
maximum loss Mrs Somaskandarajah can seek to recover is 3 month’s income as a
subpostmistress, because POL was always contractually entitled to terminate the contract on
three month’s notice for any or no reason.

Summary

of income. If however you do issue a claim, it will be met by a counterclaim of £38,898.96 plus interest,
