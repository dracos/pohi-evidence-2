Whistleblowing: Your views count

At Post Office we take your views and concerns seriously. A culture of openness
and accountability is essential to ensure Post Office matters even more tomorrow
than it does today.

That’s why we want you to complete our whistleblowing survey. We want you to
tell us what you understand about whistleblowing at Post Office. The survey results
will help us identify where improvements can be made to our whistleblowing
awareness across the business.
