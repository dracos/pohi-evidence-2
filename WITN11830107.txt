I forward this letter to confirm in writing some of the issues I verbally articulated at our
meeting on Monday 23 March. With the introduction of Causeway Project which
electronically integrates major partners in the Criminal Justice process police have been
reviewing their business processes and relationships with all agencies who have submitted
prosecution files via the police. The Purpose of this review has been to ensure optimum use

of police resources and efficiency in business processes. Traditionally Royal Mail in

The decision has now been taken by police that they should extract from the role of needless
‘middleman’ and that Royal Mail should deal directly with the PPS,

As I advised Royal Mail should liaise with PPS and expedite a protocol for submission of all
files direct to PPS,

To facilitate drafting of such agreement police will accept Royal Mail files until 1 May 2009 -
after which they will be returned to sender,

As you conceded at our meeting you had an expectation such news was pending and made
perfect business sense.

I am happy to discuss further if required or be of any assistance.
