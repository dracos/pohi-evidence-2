I had a telephone conversation with Mandy Talbot. She had received the disclosure list. She
asked me why I thought she was the most appropriate person to sign it as opposed to
somebody on the business side, or me as the solicitor dealing with it. I explained that under
the court rules, I could not sign it. I thought she was the most appropriate person to sign it
as she had overall responsibility for the claim. She did agree that she was responsible at the
Post Office for gathering all the information. She asked who had signed the pleadings. I
said that we could sign them, but what we could not do is sign a disclosure list. I said that
initially the claim had started in Chesterfield with Chery! Woodward's team, but that as it had
escalated it had been transferred to Mandy. I therefore did not know who else would be in a
Position to sign the list. Vicky Harrison at Leeds had been very helpful in assembling the
documents.

Mandy asked me whether I could say that this contained everything that Vicky had sent to
me and I said that it did. Mandy was happy to sign our list and she will sign it over, scan it
and send it back to me.

I said that tactically how I anticipated that no matter how much information we gave Mr
Castleton’s solicitors, they are bound to ask for more, because unless they get new experts
reports, that is the only real leverage they have before mediation (assuming mediation takes
place immediately after disclosure). If they get expert comment on disclosure lists, then we
could too. The point is that I think they will struggle to understand some of the financial
documents.

Informing Mandy that what I had not included was call logs post dating Mr Castleton’s
dismissal because I could not at this stage really see they were relevant. However, we can
always re-consider if Castleton’s solicitors pressed for these. Mandy agreed.

Finally explained to Mandy that Vicky Harrison had sent to me a transaction log of every
single transaction in January, February and March 2004, which was the relevant period. This
really is the full audit trail that Mr Castleton’s solicitors have been asking for, so provided
they understand that then they ought not to be asking for further documents to be on that.
