Dear Miles

Thank you for sending over the section 17 notice for Mrs Kashmir Kaur-Gill. Work has begun to collate the responsive
documents to the request to produce materials. I thought now would be a good opportunity to recap the agreed
approach to disclosure of materials.

The section 17 notices seem to contain standard wording requesting Post Office to identify and provide "all
documents and other materials in its possession in relation to the case". I wanted to remind all involved of the
discussions which took place in September - November 2015 between Post Office and the CCRC and reiterate the
document sources Post Offices searches in response to section 17 notices.

During the discussions in late 2015, both parties debated the most efficient way to collate the potentially large volume
of materials relevant to the CCRC's investigation and how best to filter the approximately 186,700 documents located
around that time so as to facilitate a targeted and proportionate review by the CCRC for current and future
investigations. With that in mind, at a meeting held on 6 November 2015, the CCRC requested that Post Office
electronic security files be filtered in relation to each individual case, to include documents containing the defendant's
surname within a particular timeframe starting 3 years before the date of conviction and ending on the date of
conviction itself.

To deliver with the targeted and proportionate materials agreed, as set out in the tracker shared with the CCRC, in
response to each section 17 notice Post Office searches for the following materials in addition to its own hard copy
security files for each relevant defendant:

Hard copy security files produced/ held by RMG;

Hard copy prosecution files produced/ held by RMG legal/ Post Office's lawyers Cartwright King,
Sift Review documents produced/ held by Cartwright King;

Electronic documents as follows:

Mediation scheme documents;

Case files;

CDs/ tapes;

Documents held by Post Office's security team or legal; and

Documents held by RMG's Security Team on their Electronic Filing Cabinet
