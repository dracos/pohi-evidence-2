POST OFFICE LIMITED v JOAN FRANCIS BAILEY
HOWEY SUB POST OFFICE
CASE NO: POLTD/1011/0164

In my opinion the evidence is sufficient to afford a realistic prospect of conviction
of the above named Defendant on the charges of fraud under the Fraud Act
2006.

In his report Steve Bradshaw the Fraud Investigator summarises the facts of the
case and the offender's personal medical problems and families medical and
unfortunate personal circumstances. From the outset the offender had made full
and frank admissions and also the nature of the offence involved and the
mitigation that is likely to be advanced on her behalf that a formal caution would
be appropriate. I also understand that the offender wished to repay all the losses
to the Business in the sum of £13,044.23 in order to do so she is in the process
of selling her house. I would therefore advise that the Fraud Investigation Officer
obtain details of the selling Agent and obtain an undertaking from the
Conveyancing Solicitor instructed by the offender on the sale from the net
proceeds the outstanding sum of £13,044.23 will be paid to Post Office Limited.
On receipt of the undertaking from the Solicitors I agree that the above named
be cautioned for the following offence:-

1 “FOR THAT YOU Joan Francis Bailey between the 1% day of August
2010 and the 5" day of January 2011 at Howey Post Office dishonestly
made false representations in the accounts namely the Final Branch
Trading Account for Howey Post Office for the period between 1% August
2010 and 5" January 2011 to the sum of £13,044.23 when you had
thereby intended to make a gain for yourself or another or to expose
Post Office Limited to a risk of loss”.

(Contrary to Section 1 of the Fraud Act 2006)
