Karen Arnold wrote to you on 3 September 2010 confirming the suspension of your
contract for services as subpostmaster of Post Office® Hightown branch. I have been
asked to take this case forward.

I have now received the necessary papers relating to this case and, after reviewing them,
should advise you that I will have to consider the summary termination of your Contract
for Services on the grounds of

1. A loss revealed at the audit of £14023.37 which after including the amount of
£819.00 Transactional Corrections was increased to £14842.37

This loss was discovered initially during the Horizon migration support visit on 1
September 2010 and confirmed during the subsequent audit on the 2 September 2010.

2. Failure to make good losses at the branch

During the audit you said that when you accepted Transaction corrections you had been
adjusting the lottery scratchcards to the correct figure but had not put in any money to
compensate for the loss.

3. Increasing the amounts of cash on hand when completing the overnight cash
holdings
