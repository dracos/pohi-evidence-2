2 Summary
In 2019 the process implemented by Post Office and Fujitsu to address issues
with Horizon was mapped. This supporting document sets out each step for
ease of review, and the process map has been updated to include step numbers
for cross-referencing. A PDF version and an editable Visio version are also
embedded for ease of reference.

Please note the Branch Resilience Manager referred to is a new role in
recruitment. The process is being managed temporarily until the new post is
filled, with an anticipated start date in early August.
