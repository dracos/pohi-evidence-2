Regina v. Alison Henderson - Ipswich Crown Court — 15 December 2010 - For Sentence

Further to my letter of 3 November, I now enclose our report upon yesterday’s proceedings. The
upshot is that the judge would not start the sentencing process until the outstanding debt due
from the defendant has been paid. For this reason no plea was actually entered, although it was

plea in his brief but my representative was not shown it.

As the judge wants to keep this case, it will (in theory) follow him to Ipswich on 15 December
but, in view of the fact that defence counsel (a Norwich barrister) does not wish to go to Ipswich,
it is really a question of “Watch this space.” Whatever happens, I shall, of course arrange
attendance upon counsel at that hearing.
