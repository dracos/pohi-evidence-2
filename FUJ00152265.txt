2 OCTOBER REVIEW OF SERVICE

2.1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY
Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for October 06 there was one Major Incident and two service affecting incidents relating to the network.

On 13" October, the Primary FTMS gateway server failed with a reported cable fault. The failover to the Secondary server was successful and the files for the
13" and 14” were sent successfully. On 15" October, a failure of the Secondary server occurred, resulting in a total loss of FTMS gateway. Service was restored
via the Secondary server on Monday 16" at 14:43. This server outage resulted in a number of file transfer failures. The original fault was cleared and both servers
restored to full functionality.

On 9" October, service to all VSAT sites was affected due to a Eurosat problem. On 23" October, an incident in the IPStream network caused congestion for up
to 400 branches —transactions were completed on retry, and remedial action was taken to address this issue.

There was one incident affecting VIP sites (Blackburn) and 4 incidents outside the Fujitsu Services domain in the month.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.67% and 99.33% respectively. OBC targets were also exceeded with all OLAs met, and the
OBC volumes reduced further to 142.

Incoming calls to the desk rose again this month, primarily driven by hardware and network calls — the network call rise was associated with the incident on 23"
October. Counter Printer calls are still the highest volumes and rose sharply towards the end of the month. Additional spares and resources are in place to address
this and the agreed remedial action plans are being continuously reviewed to ensure that the resource and spares planning for the peak trading period is correct.

Network performance levels have exceeded targets, and aside from the incident noted above, the number of network related calls remains steady.

Further review of the new service reporting has been included in this months report, and this continues, with the aim of including the agreed measures in each
area by the end of December 06. The Executive Dashboard format has been revised, and this will be reviewed at the November Service Relationship Meeting to
agree the final format going forwards.

A total of 75 complaints were received, 50 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, and only 2 were for investigation and comment. In both cases, these were
deemed to be justified, and remedial actions put in place. This significant reduction is a reflection of the additional work being done to manage the branch
expectations and communicate clearly, particularly around engineer visit times. This work continues into November, and further positive feedback is expected.

2.1.2 Availability and Problem Management

Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for October 2006 there was I Major Incident. There were 2 further service affecting incidents within
the Fujitsu domain.

There was I incident affecting a VIP site.

There were a further 4 service affecting incidents that were outside of the Fujitsu domain.

Major Incidents:

Sunday 15" October. A Failure of the Primary FTMS gateway server PHUTG124, to Huthwaite occurred on Friday 13" with a reported cable
fault. The failover to the Secondary server was successful and the files for the 13" and 14" were sent successfully. On Sunday at 14:15 a failure of
the Secondary server occurred, therefore a total loss of service to the Huthwaite FTMS gateway. Service was restored via the Secondary server on
Monday 16"" at 14:43. This server outage resulted in the inability to transfer the following files to and from POL, the file transfers included
POLMIS files, SAP-ads files, POC, & POD files, COH files, POL-FS transfer files, inbound reference data files, DRS/TES Reconciliation reports &
Bureau De Change files.

This resulted in the failure of the following service level targets: L003 Daily Cash on-hand and L005 Planned Orders. The Primary server was
restored to service on Saturday 21". Call E-0610120813 refers.

Other Service Affecting Incidents in the Fujitsu domain:

Monday 9th October. Loss of the complete VSAT (Satellite) Network, due to Satellite issues with Eurosat from Sunday. Satellite was out of
position so no network available to Hughes (VSAT service provider) at start of business Monday. Service was restored to 53 outlets by 17:00 and
the remainder of the Network 82 outlets by 18:00.

Monday 23" October. An incident occurred within the IP Stream network. A fault within BT central Links delivered into Fujitsu. Incident
affected a varying number of branches up to 400 experiencing congestion, between 08:00 to 11:00 and from 14: 20 to 14:30. Issue resolved by the
introduction of a 3“ link within BT central and branches swapped overnight to the new link. Call E-0610230036 refers.

VIP sites affected this month:
Tuesday 17" October. Blackburn VIP site 010427 (15 counter site) was unable to trade from 10:15 to 14:40. A reboot after an ADSL

communications failure rendered the site in-operable due to the site being in mid process for an OBC move. SSC & MSS were required to re-
establish the branch on ISDN by manual intervention to establish the KMA. All counter service was restored by 15:20.

2.1.3 OBC Branch Change Service Summary

In October 2006 there were a total of 142 changes undertaken, 9 of these were cancelled changes. With the exclusion of ‘Closures’ 114 changes
were undertaken. Fujitsu were responsible for 5 change requests not being completed on the target date. For Fujitsu this represents a failure rate of
1.75% against the new 4% OLA minus Closures. It also represents a failure rate of 3.76% against the original target of 5%. There were 3 line plant
failures; this represents a failure rate of 1.50% against the old 4% target excluding Line Plant. This results in a difference to the September figures
of -0.66% for the target without Closures. Compared to the September calculations, a difference of -1.29% against the 5% target rate and a
difference of -0.55% against the 4% target rate; excluding line plant.

Post Office Ltd were responsible for 3 change requests not being completed on the target date, which represents 2.26% against the 4% target, this
shows a difference against the August calculations of -0.17%.

Fujitsu’s direct failures comprised of 4 Communications and 1 Hardware. Post Office Limited direct failures comprised of 2 Compliance and 1

2.1.5 Business Continuity

During October an operational test was successfully completed for the Auto-Config service (test 8), and an operational fail-over was successfully
invoked for the Alliance and Leicester disaster recovery gateway server. A Procedural walk-through was also successfully conducted at Cable &
Wireless (test 15) for the loss of the Solutions Support and Network Management Centre facilities at Queens House Reading.

Additionally, an operational test was conducted for loss of Catalyst Switch one within the Bootle Data-centre LAN (test 6.2). This test failed to meet
the success criteria and a retest has been scheduled for the 26" of November.

Further progress has been made on revising the Business Continuity plans and test scripts for the inclusion of the NS&I warrant encashment and
MoneyGram services.

2.1.7 Horizon Service Desk and Complaints

During October, 87 complaints about Fujitsu Services were logged, though 12 of these were excluded by Post Office Ltd due to being duplicate or
inappropriate complaints, leaving a total of 75 actual complaints. Not all complaints logged are treated as complaints and many are passed back to
NBSC Advisors to be dealt with as incidents as advisors could resolve them without the need for Customer Relations intervention and investigation.

Therefore, of the complaints logged, 64 were actually treated as complaints, 4 of which were excluded as chaser / duplicate calls, resulting in 60
complaints logged against Fujitsu Services. This is a reduction of 19 complaints when compared to September's complaints data and 47 when
compared to August. The top three areas of complaint were:

Engineer complaints (including 12 about response times) - 27 Printer problems - 16 complaints, 10 of which were also complaining about the
engineering service.
On Line Services being unavailable - 11 complaints

48 of the 50 complaints passed to Fujitsu Services were for information only and of these Customer Relations logged 16 as justified complaints, all
of which were against the engineering Service / printers. 2 complaints were passed for formal investigation and comment, a significant reduction on
September's figure of 23 and August's figure of 40. Both of the complaints investigated by Fujitsu Services were justified complaints. The justified
complaints are detailed in the table below.

The significant reduction in the number of complaints logged, demonstrates the positive impact of the improvements from the branch perspective.
Whilst we continue to experience extremely high call volumes, which impacts engineer scheduling, the measures implemented last month, including
separate A priority management & conference calls have been successful in reducing the number of missed visits. In November the HSD will
implement a new call script which aims to improve the branch expectation of when an engineer will arrive, and also will remove the necessity for
branches to repeat corrective actions which they have already attempted, prior to contacting the branch.

Due to unprecedented call volumes on counter printers in October, there is the possibility of an increase in the number of complaints about this
piece of hardware in November. Fujitsu Services have sourced extra printers to mitigate against spares shortage & have implemented conference
calls and improved reporting to track calls logged against this component, which will allow focused management of the engineering scheduling and
visits.

Focus is also on identifying Service improvements for the intermittent online services faults. An analysis of all the intermittent faults for July /
August has been completed and work is underway to determine if the resolution can be improved.

4.3 KEY CROSS DOMAIN PROBLEM UPDATE

The RAG status for the key cross domain problems are based on the criteria —

Business Impact Bl score Root Cause RC score

High (H) >20 outlets affected 3 Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place 3

Medium (M) >1 <20 outlets affected 2 Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed or 2
workaround in place

Low (L) <1 outlet affected 1 Fix implemented and problem being monitored 1

RAG status = BI score x RC score = (RED = 6-9) (AMBER = 3-6) (GREEN = 1-2)
The differentiator to be applied between a RED & AMBER RAG status score of 6 depends on whether the RC is known and whether a workaround
is in place i.e. if a the score of 6 is derived by a “Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place = RC score of 3” then the RAG will

be red.

If the RC score was derived by “Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed or a workaround in place = RC score of 2 then
the RAG will be amber.
