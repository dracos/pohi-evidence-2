REPLY

1. Save insofar as it consists of admissions or save as hereinafter
appears the Claimant joins issue with the Defendant upon its
Defence.

2. It is admitted that by an Acknowledgement of Appointment dated
30" January 2004 the Claimant accepted an appointment as sub-
post-master for the Bowburn Post Office for reward on the
standard terms of contract for that position.

3. It is admitted that clause 1.1 of the Contract provides that “The
contract is a contract for services and consequently the
Subpostmaster is an agent and not an employee of Post Office
Counters Limited”. It is further admitted that clause I of section 12
of the Contract provides that “The Subpostmaster on taking up his
appointment is supplied with suitable Post Office stock, the amount
of which is fixed by the Regional General Manager” and that
clause 2 of Section 12 of the Contract provides that “The
Subpostmaster is permitted to hold Post Office Counters Ltd cash
normal to meet payments at his sub-office after due allowance has
been made for expected receipts and in accordance with official
accounting and security instructions.”

4. Save that subparagraph (6) should refer to clause 13 of Section 12,
paragraph 5 of the Defence is admitted.
