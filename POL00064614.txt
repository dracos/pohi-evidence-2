cash held at the Post Office branch was correct.

postmaster who allowed him access to the secure area. A count of the cash took place and revealed a
shortage of £536. The balance snapshot (SB/3) showed a nett discrepancy shortage of £2569.19. These

purpose of this audit was to verify financial assets due to Post Office Ltd®. At the end of the audit a
report was produced detailing the findings at Birkenshaw Post Office branch (DW/1).

A P32 balance was constructed and the physical cash on hand was checked against the declared cash on
the Office snapshot.
