Dear Sir

Post Office Limited -v- Yourself

We refer to our unanswered letters dated 19 October and 9 November to Rowe Cohen, who were then on
the record as being your solicitors, further copies of which we enclose for your ease of reference. We have
today received a Notice of Change of Solicitor from Rowe Cohen which indicates that you are now acting in
person and that they have ceased to act.

(1) Amended Defence and Counterclaim

(a) We are surprised that you now assert in paragraph 3 of the draft amended Defence and Counterclaim
that you did not sign the Cash Account for week 51 given that at tab 4 of our disclosure bundle there
is both a signed and unsigned copy of the cash account for that week. Who do you say signed the
Cash Account for that week if not you? Given that you signed it, why do you assert that it is not an
account behind which you are not entitled to go?

(b) You were ordered to serve your amended Defence and Counterclaim on 6 November, but have not
done so. We have written to Rowe Cohen several times to seek a signed copy. The trial of this matter
is due to commence on 4 December 2006. Please now immediately provide us with a signed copy of
the final version of your amended Defence and Counterclaim. You will also need to seek the Court’s
permission to serve it out of time

(c

Pursuant to paragraph 3 of the Order of Master Turner dated 23 October, you have permission to
make consequential amendments to your Defence and Counterclaim. The reduction of the
Counterclaim from £250,000 to £11,250 has not been made in response to anything that has changed
in the Amended Particulars of Claim. It is not consequential and as such, you do not have permission
to make that amendment. We do not object in principle to you reducing the Counterclaim (you will
need to seek the Court’s permission) provided that you do pay the Post Office’s costs consequential
upon such amendment which would include such costs as are attributable to the abandonment of the
majority of the Counterclaim, such costs to be assessed if not agreed.

(d) In the draft amended Counterclaim that you sent to us, you state that your loss of income as a sub-
postmaster is £11,250. You have not disclosed any evidence relating to mitigation of loss. Please now
do so. In particular, please confirm the amount of income that you have received and continue to
receive from all sub-postmasters who have worked at Marine Drive after you were suspended and if
not, why not and what steps you took to obtain any income.

(e) In our recent telephone conversations with Mr Turner, he said that he had no objection to us having
an extension of time to serve the Amended Reply and Defence to Counterclaim, commensurate with
the same additional time that it takes you to serve your amended Defence and Counterclaim. As
requested, please confirm this in writing.
