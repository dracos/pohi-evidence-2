Without prejudice save as to costs offer pursuant to CPR part 36
Rajinder Singh Bilkhu v Post Office Limited
Claim number: 7DH01510

We refer to our previous correspondence.

We believe that Mr Bilkhu’s particulars of claim do not plead an intelligible cause of action and are based
upon a mis-apprehension by him of his relationship with the Post Office Limited. We do not believe that
his claim will succeed and are of the view that it has no merit.

However, Post Office Limited are mindful that the costs of defending the claim to trial will be significant
and will exceed the amount of the claim. Accordingly, purely for the sake of commercial expediency and
without any admission of liability, Post Office Limited is willing to pay Mr Bilkhu the sum of £1 (one
pound) in full and final settlement of his claim. Any payment is to be made in full within 21 days of
acceptance of this offer.

The offer is subject to Mr Bilkhu providing an irrevocable written undertaking in favour of Post Office
Limited that the settlement is confidential and he must not disclose it to any person not a party save as
may be necessary for the purposes of implementation of the settlement agreement or if compelled to do
so by any Court or authority of competent jurisdiction.

This offer is available and open for acceptance for 21 days from the date of this fax and may only be
accepted after then in accordance with the rules set out in CPR part 36.

If Post Office Limited achieve a result which is equal to or better than this offer at trial, then we will ask
the Court to order Mr Bilkhu to pay indemnity costs from the date upon which the offer expired and
interest on those costs at a rate not exceeding 10% above the base rate.

Kindly acknowledge receipt. We await hearing from you.
