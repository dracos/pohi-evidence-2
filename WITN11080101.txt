We clearly stated as follows: “Can such complex systems ever be determined to operate “without defect or
error, securely, robustly and with integrity"? This is simply not possible. No complex system can be certified as
bug (defect) free.”

We therefore suggested as follows: “How then should an investigation into the integrity of Horizon proceed?
The aim should be to provide a report on the extent to which the system is fit for purpose, supports integrity and
robustness, and facilitates maintenance and upgrade. “

We continued to indicate how we intended to go about this in Phase 2, the necessary organisation and steps,
