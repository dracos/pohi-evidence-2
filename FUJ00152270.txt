1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Within the Fujitsu Services domain for March 07 there no Major Incidents, two service affecting incidents within
the Fujitsu domain, 1 incident relating to VIP sites and two further service affecting incidents outside the Fujitsu
domain. Details are given in the Incident Overview below.

The fix to address the issue from February relating to T30 Inc 1 Release 1 has been under pilot in 100
branches, and is pending joint agreement to release to live, given that there are no issues arising from the pilot.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.8% and 99.3% respectively. As in the
previous month, there were 3 days when no Solid Down calls were reported on the daily tracker, and the
positive trend is being maintained. 134 OBC changes were delivered in the month and all the OBC targets were
exceeded with all OLAs met.

Incoming calls to the desk remain high, with hardware, network and software calls all showing increases. Over
3000 Epson Counter Printers had been rolled out by the end of March, and 12296 sites are now migrated to
IPStream. Although the volume of incoming network related calls during the month has increased due to the
network migration activity, this has had little effect on availability, and resolution times have remained within
expectation. The root cause of the increase in software calls is still under investigation, but no immediate trends
have been identified.

The recovery plan for the Engineering SLTs is under renewed scrutiny, with service levels not showing the
continued improvement this month. Additional training and resource requirements have been accelerated, and
the effect will be monitored closely.

A total of 75 complaints were received, 32 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, a slight increase on the
previous month but with no major trends causing concern. 2 were for investigation and comment: - of these,
both were agreed to be justified and appropriate action has been taken.

Commentary
The number of complaints logged against Fujitsu Services in March has increased, however the number passed
for formal investigation has dropped to its lowest level since October 06.

Engineering

The Engineering Service Improvement Plan weekly conference calls continue to review A priority faults, and the
removal of the training message store from the basebuild, has lead to a reduction in build time of 30 minutes.

The Epson printer rollout programme to replace the Ithaca printers is underway and progress is continuing
according to schedule. To date 3078 new printers have been installed, which equates to 8.79% of the estate.
As this project continues the call, visit volumes and subsequently complaints volumes are all forecasted to drop.
The reduction in the number of counter printer faults and engineering visits will have a knock on benefit on
scheduling engineering visits.

Online Services

The number of complaints for Online services has increased in March, which is primarily due to the
implementation of phase II of the migration to IP stream, which has seen an increase in the number of online
services faults logged with the Service Desk. The migration is now in its concluding stages, with less than 500
still to migrate to IPStream. GSMs have been deployed in branches with migration issues to ensure service
continuity while the issue with the primary comms is resolved by BT. Branches experiencing solid down faults
are still running at extremely low levels, with on average 3 branches a day experiencing solid down faults, and
on several occasions during March, there were no branches with solid down faults.

5.1 Horizon Service Desk

The HSD saw an significant increase in the number of calls from Post Office Branches logged during March,
with 17,090 calls logged at the desk compared to 15,509 in February, which is the highest volume of calls to the
desk since Nov 06, and the third highest in the last 12months.

e All software, hardware and network call categories have increased, with the increase in network calls as
a result of the IP stream migration and due to the BAS Router failure on the 28" March.

e Software calls were high in March (3951). Investigations have identified that there was a rise in ‘System
Freeze/System Busy message calls' with 1416 logged in March. 765 of these were down to a ‘System
Freeze/System Busy message during the processing of a transaction. 764 were resolved by a reboot.
Further analysis will be undertaken to identify if there is a trend in certain transaction type that could be
causing the majority of the freezes.

5.2 Engineering Service

March 07 has seen the engineering call volumes rise to just over 6000 calls with slight increases in numbers of
back office printer, Pin Pad and Screen calls. Compared to March last year, calls for mobile units and back
office printers have increased significantly. The Epson counter printer rollout has so far seen 3078 new printers
installed into 419 branches.

Compared to the previous month, March has seen a slight decrease in SLT conformance. The A priority within 4
hour fix going down 0.9% to 76.6% and the C priority by 18:30 next day calls also going down 1.9% to 92.9%.
There were notable impacts on engineering service from the IP Stream project, OTI downtime and a minor
resource management issue in week three. Additional engineers, desk staff, and service management heads
are being recruited. OTI hardware is being replaced, with new OTI software currently in software development.
IP Stream issues have been raised with BT.

Base unit builds remain the single highest point of failure for A priority conformance. The Service Improvement
Plan remains in place and is reviewed weekly to address issues affecting SLA conformance. Recent success
has seen the average base unit build times reduce by up to half an hour as a result of the training message
store adjustment.

March has again seen all counter availability targets met with all conformances in excess of 99%.

5.3. Branch Network Services / Central Network

Branch Network Services
During February there were 1223 calls logged across all Network Types into the HSD.
SLT's

The ‘Priority A- 6 hours’ SLT saw 41 failures from a total of 1153 closed calls measured. The 97.7%
achievement is a decrease over the past 2 months. A contributing factor to the SLT conformance miss can
also be attributed to a number of issues resulting from IP Migration. These issues have been flagged to BT via
Service Implementation.

There is no longer a C priority measure as all Network related calls are prioritised as “A” priority.

After investigation into the failure of A priority conformance the majority of fails have been attributed to delays in
call progression within the HSD/CMT environment or with FMS engineering, with the IPStream Migration being
a major contributory factor.

Other issues in March

Due to Migration activity a higher than expected volume of extended issues were received and identified. After
extensive investigation we have identified a number of issues within the BT Infrastructure domain. We are
currently working closely with all Internal SDU’s and BT directly to resolve.

Central Network

During last month there was a major incident affecting BAS router BMGV02-R10-004 on the C&W ADSL
DataStream network. This caused a slight increase in the total number of network incidents. The bulk
migrations to ADSL IPStream have reduced the number of branches using this BAS router from a previous high
of 1,020 down to 89 at the time of the incident. The migrations are expected to be complete in the next few
months after which time the ADSL DataStream network will be fully decommissioned.

There were 34 Reconciliation incidents cleared during March. This is 8 less than that reported in February 07.

Reconciliation issues in March

e Branch 335642 (Britton Ferry) had a gateway swap on 20th February, after which the PM logged calls
stating that he was missing all transaction details performed on the G/W counter the previous day.

was sent to triage in error (where it was decommissioned) instead of the SSC. The process and

personnel to prevent a reoccurence.

e Because of the base unit error, we did not have any record of transactions done on node 1 (gateway
counter) from 08:570n 19-Feb until 15:30 on 20-Feb, when the reboot finally occurred.

Branch receipts and reports printed off before the gateway swap.

to this MID in the EMIS file.

passing on data regarding this MID in the EMIS file.

January 07. This is being closely monitored by SSC and 2 more incidents were reported in March. Branch
116136 on 06/03/07 and branch 126137 was reported by POL on 28/03/07.

No correction is needed at the branches but it may need a journal entry to correct cash in pouches within
POLFS. There is a fix for some extra diagnostics going live later this month that will help with the
investigations.
