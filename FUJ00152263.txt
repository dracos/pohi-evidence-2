2.1.2. OBC Branch Change Service Summary

In August 2006 there were a total of 304 changes undertaken; this increase was due to the Counter Reduction Programme. 4 of these were cancelled changes. With the exclusion
of ‘Closures’ 268 changes were undertaken. Fujitsu were responsible for 9 change requests not being completed on the target date. For Fujitsu this represents a failure rate of
3.36% against the new 4% OLA minus Closures. It also represents a failure rate of 3.00% against the original target of 5%. There were no line plant failures; this also represents
a failure rate of 3.00% against the old 4% target excluding Line Plant. This results in an improvement against the July figures of 5.34% for the target without Closures.
Compared to the July calculations an improvement of 3.02% against the 5% target rate and an improvement of 3.02% against the 4% target rate, excluding line plant.

Post Office Ltd were responsible for 2 change requests not being completed on the target date, which represents 0.67% against the 4% target, this also shows an improvement
against the July calculations of 3.09%.

Fujitsu’s direct failures comprised of 4 Communications, 2 Process, 2 Software and 1 Hardware.

There were 2 joint failures

Fujitsu OBC Failures Are:

South Nutfield — Cable & Wireless did not meet the request for service date for provision of the ADSL service on the PSTN line, thus delaying opening by 7 working days.
Ovenden — A fault on the ISDN line delayed the opening by 3 days whilst BT resolved.

Enfield CTO (1) — FMS engineer was late arriving to branch and the PO Ltd representative wanted to leave early resulting in a second visit to complete and a miss by I day.
Enfield CTO (2) — Engineer was informed late by the FMS desk of the need for attendance and therefore arrived at branch with insufficient time to complete the task. Re-
attendance required which resulted by a miss of 1 day.

Broadfield — ISDN line fault delayed opening by 7 days, BT engineer had failed to complete installation work although he had “completed” his job on BT system.

East Grinstead - Engineer unable to configure new gateway as SMC were unable to “clear lan” to enable build to start, resolution of software issue meant a delay of 2 days.
Swinderby — An incorrect comms number had been supplied into OCMS which meant a Gateway configuration failure. An OCR was raised to correct the number which took 3
calendar days to run through OCMS, delay to this reopening was I working day.

Brixham — Gateway failed to build on ADSL, OCR needed to change comms number in OCMS to ISDN as this was a Strategic branch, engineer re-attended with ISDN
Gateway, thus delayed by 2 working days

Cross Hands — ADSL service ordered against an incorrect PSTN number thus delaying opening by I working day.

Corrective Actions
All outstanding orders for Remote Connect ADSL (Datastream) have been cancelled and the orders resubmitted for IPStream, these have been supplied successfully

Closer working relationship with FMS re the allocation of engineering resource and OBC have re-designed the information spreadsheet to give FMS rolling 7 day notification of
all changes requiring Fujitsu engineering attendance.

2.1.3 IP Stream Migration

Phase I of the IPstream Migration has proved to be a success with 3337 branches migrating from ADSL to IPstream and 1256 migrating from ISDN to IPstream.
The Phase 1 “tail” management will be completed by 30" September with any remaining ISDN to IPstream branches transferring into Phase 2 of this project.

Work has begun on the planning of Phase 2 which is due to commence around October this year.

2.1.4 Icon Service

No update.

2.1.6 Business Continuity

During August the procedural walk-throughs for the Web Services (APOP, DVLA and PAF) and the Debit Card/Electronic Top-up Services were successfully completed. The
operational test for the Post Office Limited Northern Data-centre was also successfully completed.

The installation and commissioning of equipost masterent at LEWO2 has progressed, however the installation of Tivoli servers is taking significantly longer than originally
expected and is having an adverse effect upon the completion dates. The operational verification of LEW02, CP4126, has been retargeted for the T30 release and is now expected
to be conducted during late 2006.

2.1.8 Horizon Service Desk and Complaints

In August, 110 complaints against Fujitsu Services were passed to POL Customer Relations, 13 of which were excluded as chaser / duplicate calls. Of the remaining 97
complaints the top three areas of complaint were:

Engineer complaints (including 15 about response times) — 33
Printer problems - 30 complaints, 19 of which were also complaining about the engineering service.
On Line Services being unavailable - 25 complaints

57 of the 97 complaints were passed to Fujitsu Services for information only, and 40were passed for investigation and comment. Of those investigated by Fujitsu Services, 9
were justified complaints and 1 complaint is still outstanding (8 against engineering, 1 against the HSD & 1 against online Services). The justified complaints are detailed in the
table below.

Engineering Service / Counter Printers: Fujitsu Services is continuing the work started in previous months to improve the scheduling of engineering visits, and the availability of
spare Counter Printers. New escalation and communication procedures have been put into place between the HSD & Engineering, to ensure that the desk have the most current
information to pass to the POST MASTERs. We are also monitoring the number of missed visits and have seen volumes drop toward the end of August.

The actions scheduled for implementation in July / August, including the introductions of extra spare printers, the training of additional third party staff to increase turnaround of
the replacement printers and the fix For the sofiware fault have all been completed. Additionally the "instruction labels" for printing Postal Orders have been distributed, all of
which have seen a slight improvement in the number of Counter Printer faults logged, however visit volumes are still significantly in excess of the forecast levels.

2.1.9 Business Incident Management
There were 15 reconciliation incidents during August. This is 16 less than that reported in July 2006.

All 15 reconciliation incidents were within the 5 day response SLT.
There were no 8 hour response SLT incidents.

2.1.10 Engineer Services — Branch Visits By Engineer

The volume of branch visits by engineers remains high through August, and the volumes for components are indicating consistent trends. The
exception being for monitors, which have seen an increase attributable to mains adapter failures.

It is encouraging that Counter Printers have remained consistent and is further evidence that measures adopted to address previous high volumes in
this area continue to be effective.

Engincer on-site time have improved for the newly deployed baseline PINPADs and a similar trend is expected for base units through September
and October.

3.2.2 ONLINE NETWORK SERVICES

This month the HSD received a total of 1302 inbound network services calls. The call breakdowns show a very stable incident rate in the Fujitsu
and the online transaction provider domains. The volume of ISDN2 faults is decreasing due to ADSL migrations.

Network banking ADSL counter authorisation times have remained consistent throughout the month which supports our testing to demonstrate that
the ADSL IPStream service would be of equivalent speed to the existing ADSL DataStream service.

The MTFR on the ADSL DataStream and VSAT services continue to be erratic but with most faults on the PSTN lines there were only 21
qualifying incidents producing these results. The MTFR on the ISDN2 service increased to 16.90 hours and service improvement actions are in
