determinative.

Castleton was willing to consider mediation before disclosure. Accordingly, on 6 March we sent to

directions.

6. In terms of financial documents, we anticipate that our disclosure list will include items such as

7. The costs involved pursuing this matter to trial, given the number of witnesses and experts, will be
significant and will certainly significantly exceed the amount in dispute. The longer you wait to
mediate, the more costs both parties will incur and given the financial stakes will be higher, we
anticipate that it may well prove harder to settle. We say this particularly for the benefit of your
client, because we believe that the Post Office has a very strong case. If Mr Castleton reaches the

8. We have serious concerns about the merits of Mr Castleton’s defence and counterclaim. For

9. If parties could never meaningfully mediate until after disclosure, then CPR 44.5(3a)(ii) would not

settled before the disclosure stage.
