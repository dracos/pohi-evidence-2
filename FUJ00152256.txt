2.1.2 Availability and Problem Management

Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for February 2006 there was 1 Major Incident. There was no further, service affecting incidents within
the Fujitsu domain, there were 4 incidents affecting VIP sites. There were 2 further service affecting incidents which were outside of the Fujitsu domain.

Major Incidents:

Wednesday 8" February. Duc to the expiry of a KMA security key, the DCS and ETU Services experienced 100% transaction timeouts across the
whole Post Office Limited Infrastructure. This was from the start of business day until 10.42 hrs. This has been the subject of an MI Report, detailing
what happened, root cause analysis and corrective actions.

Other incidents in Fujitsu Services domain:

VIP sites affected this month.

Wednesday 8" February. VIP Site 011704 Belfast City Centre loss of on-line services from 09.39 hrs to 10.04 hrs, a reboot of the system resolved the
Thursday 9" February. VIP Site 014323 The Markets Leeds, down from start of day until 14.51 hrs, required a gateway swap after reversion back to
Monday 13" February. VIP site Sunderland FAD 004343, H/W failure required a Gateway replacement. No service available at outlet from 09:17 to
Tuesday 14" February. VIP site Londonderry FAD 004711, no on-line services available, required a re-boot of the gateway. No service available from
09:37 to 10:33.

Incidents Outside the Fujitsu Domain:
Tuesday 7" February. There was a loss of the DVLA service, from start of day until 10.40 hrs due to the DVLA Servers being unavailable
Tuesday 7" February. Barclays Timeouts from 08.33 hrs to 09.33 hrs showing 10% failures, due to a problem between LINK and Barclays.

2.1.3. OBC Branch Change Service Summary

February 2006 realised further sustained achievement from Fujitsu Services OBC in delivering a performance within the OLA targets. A total of 242
changes were received, 13 of these were cancelled changes. With the exclusion of ‘Closures’ 204 changes were undertaken. Fujitsu were responsible
for 5 change requests not being completed on the target date. For Fujitsu this represents a failure rate of 2.45% against the new 4% OLA minus
Closures. It also represents a failure rate of 2.18% against the original target of 5%. There were no line plant failures therefore this also represents a
failure rate of 2.18% against the old 4% target excluding Line Plant. This is an extremely minimal difference of -0.04% compared to January’s Target
without Closures, also compared to the January calculations a difference of -0.02% against the 5% target rate and -0.02% against the 4% excluding
Line Plant target rate.

Post Office Ltd were responsible for 6 change requests not being completed on the target date, which represents 2.62% against the 4% target, which
shows a difference against the January calculations of -1.42%.

Corrective Actions

Fujitsu continues to work closely with Cable & Wireless to minimise the materialisation of issues surrounding provision of communications.

Recent hardware failings are being addressed by the Fujitsu OBC Service Delivery Manager with Triage to further try and eliminate transportation
damage and the arrival of faulty hardware to site. Alternative delivery specialists are used in some instances to try and ensure kit arrives safely. Regular
feedback is also given to TNT to aid them in improving the service they deliver for Fujitsu.

The new RAG status for the key cross domain problems are based on the criteria —

Business Impact

>1 <20 outlets affected

2

<1 outlet affected

1

Fix implemented and problem being monitored

1

RAG status = BI score x RC score = (RED = 6-9) (AMBER = 3-6) (GREEN = 1-2)
The differentiator to be applied between a RED & AMBER RAG status score of 6 depends on whether the RC is known and whether a workaround is in
place i.e. ifa the score of 6 is derived by a “Root cause unknown, no progress or no workaround in place = RC score of 3” then the RAG will be red.

If the RC score was derived by “Root cause determined, implementation agreed/needs to be agreed or a workaround in place = RC score of 2 then the

RAG will be amber.
