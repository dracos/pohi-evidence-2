Post Office Ltd v Alison Loraine HALL
Case No. POLTD/1011/0095

In answer to your questions;

Please find a copy of the Audit report at Appendix ‘c’
The Branch Trading statements have been forwarded to you
previously.

From my conversations and contact with P&BA in Chesterfield
they cannot see any reason for the branch loss being caused by
the Lottery. Mr Neil Thorneycroft, Lottery Team Leader, has
confirmed this in his email dated 8‘ September 2010 in which
he states;

“I have looked at this office again and I can’t find errors
for the amounts in question. Over the last 2 years we’ve
issued the following.

National Lottery Game : 03.04.08 to date £1,052.00 credit
Prize Payments: 24.04.08 to date £190.20 debit

Scratchcards: 23.06.08 to date £2,200.00 debit ( stock credit
)

Regular numbers of apparent scratchcard sales entries have
been made. However a high number of stock adjustments have
also been made. From 01.01.08 to date 115 stock adjustments @
£1760.00 credit. If stock adjustments have been made in place
of sales entries no commission is payable to the Postmaster.”

I have no information to say that an audit was requested by
the Postmaster and from my initial report it can be seen that
the clerk, Miss Kimberley Langley, was told by Miss Hall to
add a specific figure to the Cash Declaration in August 2010
when she was running the office in Miss Hall’s absence. So I
would suggest there would have been ample opportunity to
request and Audit if Miss Hall considered the loss was not her
doing.

A copy of the transaction corrections is now at Appendix ‘Cc’
again supplied by Mr Thorneycroft. Should this matter progress
to Court I will obtain a Statement from him detailing his
finding.

These papers are now submitted for advice.
