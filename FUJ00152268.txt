1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Within the Fujitsu Services domain for January 07 there no Major Incidents, one service affecting incident within
the Fujitsu domain and 1 incident relating to VIP sites. Details are given in the Incident Overview below.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.7% and 99.2% respectively. 97 OBC.
changes were delivered in the month and all the OBC targets were exceeded with all OLAs met.

Incoming calls to the desk were at the expected level for the month, with hardware calls continuing at a high
daily rate, but steady. The incoming call volumes continue to exceed the agreed levels and the associated SLTs
have been recalculated accordingly.

Work continues to establish the effect of the replacement mail labels on the counter printer call volume.
However the call volume remains high. The recovery plan for the Engineering SLTs is progressing slowly and
the service levels have improved, but still give cause for concern. Joint work continues with all parties to build
on and sustain the recovery, with workshops and Sense and Respond activities to support the action plans.

Progress is being made on improving the process for network fault resolutions, and an all-parties meeting was
held, with follow up actions from BT to be pursued and a further meeting to be held in March.

Commentary

The number of complaints logged against Fujitsu Services remains constant, with numbers investigated by
NBSC Customer Relations remaining around 60, with only 3 complaints passed to Fujitsu Services for formal
investigation, compared to 10 in December. There has been a reduction in the number of complaints in all top 3
areas.

Engineering

The Engineering Service Improvement Plan continues to be the subject of focus, with weekly conference calls
and Sense & Respond® analysis, particularly into the A priority faults. Setting branch expectation continues to
be a focus area, and work is underway with Post Office to review communications to the network.

Despite minor supplier problems in January there was a slight decrease in the number of complaints logged for
counter printers. Analysis is continuing into the root cause of the increase in counter printer faults, believed to
be a change in the label specification, which is being investigated by Post Office Ltd and Royal Mail Group.

Online Services

January saw the continuation of low volumes of solid down branches although there was a slight increase in the
number of intermittent faults, caused by weather impacting the BT infrastructure. A meeting was held with BT,
Post Office Ltd, Openreach & Fujitsu Services and the fault resolution processes and key issues were
discussed. BT took away a number of actions and will propose potential solutions at a second meeting in
March 07.

5.2 Engineering Service

January 07 has seen engineering service calls remain in excess of 6,000. The number of Ithaca calls has
remained consistent with the previous 3 months, and we have seen a substantial increase in the number of
keyboard and pin pad replacements during the month. Compared to January 2006 these figures are typical and
can be attributed to Branches ensuring faults are repaired after the busy December period. We expect to see a
reduction in February to the more normal levels of September/October last year.

Despite the high volumes, January has seen a steady increase in SLT conformance over December with the A
priority 4 hour target up by nearly 7% at 68.9% and the C priority 6 hour target up by more than 4% to 92.4%
The removal of counter printer faults from the latter failure would improve this to 94.9%.

To address the performance of A priorities there is now a specific service improvement plan in place, among the
items being addressed are the build times of base units. A CP is being raised to impact the removal of the
training application from the Horizon build, thus reducing the size of the messagestore and improving the build
time for re-configurations. Work is also under way to reduce the OTI transfer times of calls thus giving the
engineering workforce more of the 4 hour SLT to attend and resolve.

Both the Branch and Counter availability targets for the month were conformant.

5.3. Branch Network Services / Central Network

Branch Network Services
During December there were 1,115 calls logged across all Network Types.

The ‘Priority A- 6 hours’ SLT saw 19 failures from a total of 1,001 closed calls measured. The 98.1%
achievement was the same as for December.

The ‘Priority C - 6 hours’ SLT saw 2 failures from 50 calls — an achievement of 96%.

After investigation into the failures above it has been identified that the contributing factors were related to the
HSD call handling and delays in engineering attendance. Both of these issues are being addressed with the
appropriate SDU's. We are currently in preparation of moving the onus of managing any Network related
incident into the CMT (Comms Management Team). This will allow the team to directly focus their efforts on
progression of each individual incident and manage to resolution. This will include more focus on call handling
and engineering attendance. It is expected that these changes will take effect from 26/02/07.

Other issues in January

We have continued to receive a high number of ADSL/IP service cease notifications being processed by BT due
to Branch Manager's activity. We are continuing to progress this issue with assistance from POL, C&W and
internal SDU’s.

Central Network

This month a total of 1,115 network calls were received at the HSD. The average network banking transaction
times have improved slightly reflecting the migration of slower ISDN and VSAT branches to the faster ADSL
IPStream and VSAT-BB networks. There has also been good progress on several internal projects to simplify
and improve the central network.

January 2007

5.6 Data Centre Operations Service / Data File Transfer

There were no major incidents in relation to the Data Centre Operations Service within the Data Centre central
Infrastructure for the month of January 07.

Throughout the month there was a total of 185 operational changes, 169 made by Post Office Account and 16
by Post Office Limited 3” parties, there were no service impacts as a result of those changes being
implemented

The final electrical maintenance work has been completed in the Bootle Data Centre, with no service impact to
the live service during the planned activity.

Currently there are 20 customer facing risks identified in the Data Centre Operations service classified by RAG
status as 2 amber and 18 green risks.

There are two key activities taking place during the month of February, there is the planned migration from
frame relay circuits to IP Select circuits for TIP gateways within the Wigan and Bootle Data Centres and
Huthwaite.

The second activity is the planned decommission of the EMC Centerra generation one disks arrays which are
used as part of the audit/ prosecution service and will be replaced with generation four arrays.

5.6.1 Day J Failures

DFD Outbound figures were impacted by a total of 3 No Lets this month. This resulted in 2 Day J targets failing,
however the APS Outbound Day J target passed as there were no APS transactions from any of these 3
Branches during the down periods detailed below.

203859 — 14 Days late — Problem still ongoing

This outlet is only open on Mondays from 15:00 to 17:00. A call was logged on 16th January, however the PM
was un-contactable for a visit until the 29th. The engineer attended on 29th January and identified that DR could
not be performed because the gateway was down. The engineer subsequently had to order a luggable. The PM
then suggested that the engineer could not return until 5th February due to holidays. This issue is still ongoing
due to spares problems concerning the luggable. Whilst we endeavour to ensure that luggables are
strategically placed there is still a recognised shortage. The PHU2 is currently being developed and a CP has
been initiated to this effect. It is anticipated that the PHU2 solution will replace a percentage of Luggable
Branches thus increasing the loop stock to FMS.

118845 — 13 Days late — Problem resolved, site now health checking

PM called with no online services on 18/12. BT were appointed to attend site on 21/12 and finally resolved an
underground cable fault on 27/12. The call then went to FMS who did various kit replacements and resolved on
24/1. DR Call logged, but due to spares issues FMS had to wait for original fault to be resolved on 24/1.

209840 10 Days late —- Problem resolved, site now health checking

This fault was first reported on 20" January. The engineer was unable to attend until 26" as spares in the

area were on back order. Whole Unit Spare Luggable arrived on 26th and engineer fitted, however a further
hardware fault became evident and a replacement Luggable screen was required, this was then ordered and a
fix was completed on 30/1. During this time a Day J DR could not be completed due to the BU being faulty.
This site is in Inverness and whilst we endeavour to ensure that Luggables are strategically placed there is still
a recognised shortage. The PHU2 is currently being developed and a CP has been initiated to this effect. It is
anticipated that the PHU2 solution will replace a percentage of Luggable Branches thus increasing the loop
stock to FMS

5.7 Reconciliation Service

There were 21 Reconciliation incidents cleared during January.

This is 3 less than that reported in December 06.

There was only 1 priority incident and that was cleared within the 8 hour response SLT.
The remaining 20 incidents were cleared within the 5 day response SLT.

5.8 Reference Data & Message Broadcast Service

RDT noticed in mid January that the HR SAP calendar was about to expire and there was no indication that
POL were taking steps to provide a new calendar. It was later found that POL were under the impression that
they didn’t need to provide the calendar until towards the end of the first missing period.

POL then tried to submit a calendar which would have created a Period 13 but the system had never been
proven for this period and it was found that the logic assumes that there are only 12 periods in any year.
Eventually new data was provided and released to the Live estate.

There may be a need to handle a period 13 next year but if this is required it will almost certainly require a
change request. We will be looking to POL to provide the calendar in 2008 a little earlier.

Nothing to report on MBS

Manual Key Refresh

There were no manual keys generated this month, but the Moneygram password was successfully changed on
the 25th Jan.
