Mr Todd gave his report, Mr Riesenfeld presented
on the 1996 financial performance, 1997 budget
and forecast, and the Board reviewed the draft
1996 Statutory Reports and Accounts. The
following points were noted:-

a) The expected 1996 loss before tax of £2.5m was
disappointing, but was a considerable improvement
from the expectation in November, principally as
a result of profit on disposals, provision
releases, and the UK Pension Plan surplus (see
below).

forward as a systems and services Group, with
demerger of non-core activities, the financial
performance was disappointing. Actions were
being taken in 1997 to learn the lessons from
1996, including improved project Management,
ending rationalisations, more customer focused
activity, more value pricing, better alignment of
people resources to business opportunities and
more aggressive purchasing.

97/4 PRESENTATION BY MS MARIE-ANNE VAN INGEN ON Tplc

Ms van Ingen presented on her business, ICL’s
distributor and reseller of leading brands of
small/medium servers, PCs and other products.
Amongst matters covered were the potential of
direct electronic purchasing and the Centurion
Project to supply 100,000 Fujitsu/ICL PC and
server units in the UK in 1997 (where Ms van
Ingen led not only her business but other ICL and
Fujitsu family operations). Planned 1997 revenue
was £355m and profit £4.5m. The 1998 plan was
£385m revenue and £8.7m profit. The business was
going well and these figures might be achieved
early.

97/5 PRESENTATION BY MR NR HARTNELL ON ICL SERVICES

Mr Hartnell presented on his business, covering
consulting, systems integration, and IT and
business process outsourcing. The business
included ICL CFM (which now included all ICL’s
outsourcing activities), ICL Network Services
(which operated in a buoyant market and had an
aggressive plan) and ICL Education & Consultancy
(which included the Peritas unit - winner of
awards as number one training company in the UK).
Revenues achieved from these businesses in 1996
had been £319m and operating profit £14m.

97/6 HEALTH & SAFETY POLICY STATEMENT - PLC/97/5

Mr Todd said that following the discussion at the
last meeting of the Board, the proposed Health &
Safety Policy statement had been reviewed to
reflect fully up to date UK practice and was also
aligned as accurately as possible with ICL’s
liability in this field. The policy made no
mention of reduction of stress at work,
particularly because that could not be measured,
but Mr Todd would cover stress in a separate
instruction.

The Board approved the statement and authorised
the Chief Executive to sign it. ----------,

97/9 ACQUISITIONS, DIVESTMENTS

Mr Todd mentioned discussions concerning possible
divestment of all or part of ICL’s remaining
interests in South Africa. He was asking those
putting together plans for a management buy-out
to make an offer fairly soon, or ICL would
withdraw from discussions. Mr Gardener said he
believed conditions in South Africa were
improving and there were continued opportunities
for ICL there. Mr Todd said that Mr Gardener’s
contacts would be followed up. Sir Peter said
that this must rank fairly low on ICL’s
priorities at this time.
