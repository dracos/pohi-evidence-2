1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Within the Fujitsu Services domain for November 06 there no Major Incidents and three service affecting
incidents relating to the network.

Friday 3° November. An outage on the IPStream network affected up to 1000 branches during a 20 minute
outage. This was due to an unscheduled change and was regressed immediately. The process for such
changes has been addressed.

Thursday 16" November. An IPStream Router fault caused up to 950 branches to disconnect. Most branches
started reconnecting immediately but all were back on-line within 30 minutes. Corrective action has been
completed to address the root cause.

Monday 27" November. An incident occurred on a BAS Router affecting 157 branches. Root cause
investigation is still on-going with Cable & Wireless.

There were no incidents affecting VIP sites and 1 incident outside the Fujitsu Services domain in the month.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.72% and 99.23% respectively. OBC
targets were also exceeded with all OLAs met — and the OBC volumes increased slightly to 169 changes
delivered.

Incoming calls to the desk continue to rise, driven by hardware call volume. Counter Printer calls are still the
highest volumes and back office printers have also increased sharply, in line with additional usage relating to
Track & Trace. A replacement programme is under way to bring all the back office printers in line with the OKI
standard. Remedial action plans relating to counter printers are being continuously reviewed and revised to
address the ongoing issues, and keep the situation stable whilst the volume continues to rise. Engineering SLT
performance continued to be well below target, and weekly action plans and performance figures are being
reviewed across the teams to maintain a steady recovery.

Network performance levels have generally exceeded targets, and the number of network related calls remains
steady. The impact of the implementation of Branch Network Resilience can be seen in the differing profile of
incoming calls, whilst availability remains high.

This month’s report is in the agreed revised format, and the majority of new contractual measures are now
reflected in the SRB, including the new Executive Dashboard.

A total of 80 complaints were received, 39 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, and 7 were for
investigation and comment. Of these, 3 were agreed to be justified and have been addressed.

3 Business Continuity

3.1 Business Continuity Testing

During November operational business continuity tests were successfully completed for the POL-FS site fail-
over (test 28.1) and the Generic Agent server fail-over (test 7).

Procedural walk-throughs were also successfully conducted for the SMC (test 12), for which the scenario
focused upon the loss of Wigan, and for the loss of Bootle data-centre (test2).

3.2 Forthcoming Testing
There are three test activities outstanding from the proposed 2006 test schedule.

e The Reference Data operational test, which has been conducted as part of the RDT disaster recovery
implementation at LEW02 has just completed. Data from the Ref Data rig at BRAO1 was restored to the
RDT Host at LEW02 and confidence checks were successfully completed. Full verification of the data
will be completed as part of the LEW02 site verification in 2007.

e A number of factors, e.g. operational issues, new release testing requirements, platform build
complexities and build discrepancies have delayed the implementation and commissioning of the
LEW02 disaster recovery site. The operational verification of LEWO02 during 2006 has not been
possible. It is intended that this test will be conducted during 2007.

e An operational re-test of the Data-centre LAN (test 6.2) Catalyst failure.
The proposed dates for testing in January 2007 are:

¢ The 2006 operational re-test for the Data-centre LAN is the 7" of January. (This is subject to a code
correction being available.)

¢ The operational test for the HSD is planned for the 18" of January
e The operational test for the Correspondence server is planned for the 21" of January

3.3 Business Continuity Planning

Further progress was made on revising the Business Continuity plans and test scripts for the inclusion of the
NS&l warrant encashment and MoneyGram services. Version 6.0 of the Horizon Services Business Continuity
Plan (CS/PLA/079) is scheduled to be completed during December.

3.4 Business Continuity Incidents and Issues

No Major Business Continuity Incidents were raised during November. A leak of a radiator on the sixth floor at
BRAO1 was managed as a potential MBCI and a management report for this incident was produced.

The LEW02 Disaster Recovery site implementation and commissioning is taking significantly longer than
originally expected. This has primarily been due to platform build complexities, build discrepancies, operational
issues and LST resource involvement in testing new releases.

5 Service Summary

5.1 Horizon Service Desk

The HSD saw an increase in the number of calls from Post Office Branches logged during November, with
17,266 calls logged at the desk compared to 16,155 in October. The biggest increases were seen in Hardware
and PostShops calls with Software calls still running above the normal run rate. The variance in each area can
be attributed to the following:

« Hardware Calls volume due to an unprecedented upturn in the number of Counter Printer faults logged
towards the end of October and throughout November. Action has been taken under the engineering
service to ensure that there are sufficient spares & engineering resources to manage this volume of
calls.

e There has also been a 50% increase in the number of hardware faults logged on the Back Office Printer
and a near doubling in the number of Engineer visits made for OKI printers (up to 778 visits compared
to 398 during October). Reasons for the upturn include the increased usage of the Back Office Printer
due to the introduction of Track & Track, increased requests for consumables & that the branch was.
advised to turn off automatic printing of Bureau Rates. The desk resolved over 1000 of these calls.

e PostShops: There was an issue on the reports on the Geller Till, which resulted in an influx of calls to
the desk. Geller has released a fix for this issue.

* — Software Calls volume increase in October / November has been identified as an ‘EMV launch’ event
that is generated on the counter. Clicking OK to the error message allows the PM to continue working
with no adverse impact to the counter. There is a development underway to change the error
message, to prompt the PM to click OK and continue working, and the underlying root cause of the
fault is still under investigation.

Review of HSD SLTs

The calls answered within 30 seconds SLT was missed this month (75.33%), due to extremely high incoming
call volumes. Additional heads have been recruited and are currently being trained to meet the demand.

This SLT has been recalculated in accordance with section 2.3.2.1 of the Service Desk Service Description, and
the reported figure is 90.22%

Sense & Respond / Service Improvement Activities

The desk has implemented a new call script this month, in response to the comments received at the Sense &
Respond workshop held in Birmingham. This new call script aims to improve the branch expectation of when
an engineer will attend site & also removes the need for the branch to repeat diagnostic tests they have
attempted prior to calling the HSD.

5.2 Engineering Service

November has seen an increase in the number of engineering branch visits, with larger volumes of counter
printer, CTX flat screen and back office printer calls than have been experienced throughout the rest of the
year. This increase has resulted in another month of non-conformant SLT performance. Our ongoing focus on A
priority calls resulted in the improved conformance of 64.5%. The increase in printer call volumes gave us the
slightly lower conformance of 93.0% against C priority calls.

Analysis reveals that these high call volumes can be partially attributed to an increase in the number of counters
being used in the ramp up to the seasonal peak. It is also worth noting that the severe weather conditions
experienced during November had an impact (e.g. power surges) on branch hardware resulting in the need for
more engineer visits.

The introduction of Track & Trace has resulted in an increased usage of back office printers. It is reasonable to
attribute this increase to the higher than normal volumes of engineering visits. End of life, and problems caused
by power surges has caused the increase in CTX flat screen problems. To mitigate the effect of the counter
printer demand, our supplier is continually being asked to increase the production rates. This includes working
weekends and shipping over additional supply from USA.

Continuing efforts to improve service include:
e Daily conference calls to identify risks for the following day
e Increased vocal follow up/escalation for A priority calls from IMT.

e Monitoring of base unit build times — IPStream sites have seen a 7% decrease in build times to date
and this will continue as phase II of the migration progresses.

e Continued geographic scheduling of engineers allowing maximum closures per day.

e Ascheduled (early December) improvement in the link between our call management systems should
improve the quality of information passed from desk to engineer and vice versa.

e Support engineers will be allocated to assist resource working to reduce work in progress counts.
e Additional desk resource has been recruited since November to improve management of calls.

e A forthcoming engineering workshop to discuss recurring issues within engineering service such as
skills, tools, contact with PM’s, ETA’s, closures etc.

5.3. Branch Network Services / Central Network

Branch Network Services
November has seen a 50% rise in ISDN faults over October. Total November ISDN Faults 51

The rise in ISDN faults has had an impact on the Daily Tracker figures as the majority of these issues have
been rural branches where GSM backup was not available as a temporary solution.

ADSL faults have decreased by more then 50% from 57 in October to 20 in November.
IP Stream faults have remained very low with under 5 faults being raised in November.

Branch and Counter Availability have both remained above target for the month at 99.72% and 99.23%
respectively.

The ‘Priority A — 6 hours’ SLT saw 16 failures from a total of 963 calls. Although the target (100%) was missed,
the 98.3% achievement is an improvement compared with the previous 2 months.

Other issues in November

We have seen a stark increase in ADSL/IP service cease notifications being processed by BT due to Branch
Manager request. We are continuing to progress this issue with assistance from POL, C&W and internal
SDU’s.

Overall it has been a successful month with particular focus being placed on re-establishing comms to a branch
regardless of the issue ownership. This has been achieved with installation of GSM and PSTN Modems. This.
action has been taken to ensure that Post Office availability has been retained during the critical trading period
in the run up to Christmas.

Central Network

During November 2006 the HSD logged a total of 1170 network calls. This is the lowest recorded number since
January 2004. Despite this success there were 2 brief IPStream incidents on 3rd and 16th November 2006 and
a DataStream incident on 27th November 2006. Appropriate corrective actions are in place to minimise the risk
of reoccurrence.

5.6 Data Centre Operations Service / Data File Transfer

There were no major incidents in relation to the Data Centre Operations Service within the Data Centre central
Infrastructure for the month of November 06.

Throughout the month there was a total of 126 operational changes, 116 by Post Office Account and 10 by
Post-Office Limited suppliers.

There were no service impacts as a result of those changes being implemented

Following on from last month’s summary, the work has been completed in replacing the UPS batteries in both
Wigan and Bootle Data Centres with no service impact to the live service during the planned activity.

Currently there are 20 customer facing risks identified in the Data Centre Operations service classified by RAG
status as 1 red, 1 amber and 18 green risks.

There will be a further requirement to perform additional maintenance activities in the Bootle Data Centre in
January 07 which will result in the Data Centre running on secondary power (generator) for a period of eight
hours overnight.

5.6.1 Day J Failures
DFD Outbound figures were impacted by a total of 5 No Lets this month:

130828 -— 12 days late - Fault now resolved and site health-checking

ceased. Referred to POL. GSM had no signal and PSTN modem would not connect. Data retrieval could not be
performed as GSM had no signal and line was ceased. Line re-provided and service resumed 22/11.

196840 - 13 days late - Fault now resolved and site health-checking

Scourie Post Office. Online services unavailable reported 15/11. Possible one way comms, call sent to FMS.
Eng attended 16/11, swap failed @ 1%.Engineer re-attended 20/11, swap failed due to BT line fault. Call sent to

swapped faulty unit. Unable to perform Data retrieval or use GSM as base unit was faulty. Issue resolved 27/11.

433642 - 16 days late — Fault now resolved and site health-checking

Scurlage Post Office. PM reported comms issue. Call sent to BT who advised no fault. Call sent to FMS to swap
base unit, the call was returned several times by FMS without a unit swap being attempted. This was escalated
to lan Mills and Martin Hammond. BT advised that a fault was cleared on 24/11. FMS attended on the 27/11
and swapped out the unit and issue was resolved. GSM had no signal. Data retrieval call was raised but FMS
failed to carry this out within the agreed deadline. Issue resolved 27/11.

203859 — 11 days late — Fault now resolved and site health-checking

Ardentinny Post Office. Open Mondays 1500-1700 ONLY. Online services issue reported, call sent to BT on
20/11. BT attended on the 27/11 and resolved fault. No signal for GSM. Data retrieval call was raised, engineer
attended site but did not perform Data retrieval as requested. Call was sent back to FMS, who did not attend
until 4 days later on the 27/11. Issue resolved 27/11.

188869 - 18 days late — Fault now resolved and site health-checking

Kershader (storage) Post Office. Open Mondays and Fridays 1300-1500 ONLY. Pm reported faulty base unit
21/11, Engineer attended 22/11, base unit not swapped with no explanation given. FMS re-attended 27/11, unit

successfully 7/12. Data retrieval and GSM could not be performed as base unit was faulty. Issue resolved 7/12.
