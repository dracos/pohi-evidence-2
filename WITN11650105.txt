"Post Office video presentation - Subpostmaster Interviews’

20 April 2023

An open letter.

From the Postmasters.

I think you have to be a people's person to be a
good Postmaster. I would get on with anyone.

Being a Postmaster, I love to see the lonely old
people and ladies. Patience is very important,
especially being at the post office counter.

Knaphill is my village. Knaphill is my area.

I will go out of my way because I love my people and
I don't want to let anybody down.

Being a Postmaster, you get to see all sorts of
customers. You get to see all age groups as well.
There was a customer come to us one day and he say he
hasn't got money. We endup giving him bread and coffee
and stuff. We try and help where we can.

There is one lady who is -- who can't hear and who
can't see and she comes in hoping that we will help hey
and the ladies in here have always helpedher. So when
she comes in, we just have to pay a little bit more
attention to her.

now. Unfortunately, that gentleman has passed away.
