Post Office Limited -v- Mr L Castleton

Thank you for your letter dated 19 October.

It would have been helpful to have received your response to the suggested directions earlier. We have
written to you on various occasions and have also tried calling your Mr Turner previously and have left
several voice mail messages.

1. Amendments to Statement of Case

Thank you for confirming that you consent to the amendments to the Particulars of Claim and Reply
and Defence to Counterclaim.

XN

Transfer to Central London County Court
Your comments on venue are noted.

We do not accept any of your assertions in relation to loss. In particular, we question how Mr
Castleton can bring a CPR Part 20 claim on the basis that the value of the business has reduced and
there is a loss of profit arising from the removal of the Post Office, given that it is common ground that
the Post Office has not in fact been removed.

3. Exchange of Witness Evidence

We would prefer exchange of witness evidence to take place by Friday 27 October 2006. We are
conscious that the trial period will commence on 4 December 2006.

4. Expert Forensic Accountancy and IT Evidence

It is unclear how Mr Castleton says in fact the losses did not occur and you now admit that Mr
Castleton “is unable to say at this point how the system is flawed.” You state that Mr Castleton’s
forensic accounting expert witness “hopes” to be in a position to finalise his report for just week 42 by
Friday 3 November. You agree that sequential exchange of expert's reports is desirable so that our
experts can then deal with specific allegations, but suggest that the Post Office’s experts consider the
week 42 evidence, report back on it and then the whole exercise is if necessary repeated for week 43
etc.

Your experts have by now had ample opportunity (several months) to prepare their reports. Indeed,
they were due for exchange by 13 October 2006. This litigation cannot continue indefinitely. Our view
is either that the Court should not now allow you to adduce any late expert evidence and the
December trial should go ahead, but if late evidence is permitted, then:

(a) You should serve all expert evidence that you intend to rely upon at the same time. There
appears to be no reason to focus just on Cash Account week 42 to the exclusion of other weeks,
