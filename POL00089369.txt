INDICTMENT

IN THE CROWN COURT AT CHESTER

THE QUEEN -V- GRANT ALLEN

GRANT ALLEN is charged as follows:

Count 1

STATEMENT OF OFFENCE

Fraud, contrary to S.1 of the Fraud Act 2006

PARTICULARS OF OFFENCE

GRANT ALLEN between the 1* of April 2010 and the 7 of
February 2012,dishonestly and intending to make a gain for
yourself or another, or to expose another to risk of loss, made
representations to Post Office Ltd, which were and which you
knew were untrue or misleading, namely that the Winsford Post
Office had more cash on the premises than was actually the case
resulting in a shortage of £11,705, in breach of section 2 of the
Fraud Act 2006.
