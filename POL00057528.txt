POST OFFICE LTD SECURITY
INTERIM / CURRENT STATUS REPORT

POLTD/1112/0007 - Rowlands Castle 21st March 2012

This case is regarding the former Subpostmaster Mrs Lynette Hutchings and a full
précis of the investigation can be seen from the earlier reports and tape summaries
previously paginated within this file. Original exhibits, statements and unused
material are associated with file.

Mrs Hutchings has been summonsed to appear at Portsmouth Magistrates Court at
14.30 hrs on Tuesday 10 April 2012. Please note that I have looked on Track and
Trace and the summons does not appear to have been delivered as the item is
shown as being processed through the system. From enquiries made it is believed
that Mrs Hutchings no longer resides at this address, although I have not had
chance to try and establish her current whereabouts.

In view of the fact that the court and Cartwright King have been advised that the
summons has been served (posted by Special Delivery) then fairly urgent steps
need to be taken in order to establish her whereabouts and a copy of the summons
served on her. If her whereabouts are not established prior to 10 April 2012 then
the court and Cartwright King need to be advised and the hearing removed until
such time as she is located and a new date arranged with the court.

To date no money has been repaid by Mrs Hutchings. There is no involvement
from the FIU in respect of this case.

This case file was transferred to Steve Bradshaw, Security Manger on 21 March
2012 for allocation to a new Security Manager in order to locate Mrs Hutchings,
serve the summons and oversee the prosecution process.

Graham Brander
