John

I had a meeting with Ernie Waterworth in May 2013. The background to cases investigated by the Post Office was
that they would be submitted to PPS by police and prosecuted by the relevant Region- usually as a theft or

fraud. Mr Waterworth met with me to explain that Post Office wanted to cut out using PSNI as basically a postbox,
and submit files directly to PPS. He has been retained by Post Office Limited to in effect quality assure files
investigated before they are submitted to PPS. I had explained to him that this was possible and that cases could be
sent directly to PPS in hard copy format. This would in effect add Post Office Limited to the list of Departments we
prosecute for. I explained to Mr Waterworth that we would have to charge for cases as we were only funded for
PSNI work. There has been no SLA drawn up or entered into.

Mr Waterworth submitted a file over the summer which I believe you are aware of John as there was some query as
to whom the correct complainant should be. I note that the case in question here is one of the cases previously
submitted via PSNI which Caroline Hamilton directed on.

In general the Departmental SLAs are not deemed to cover confiscation. When Ann was in HCI this query arose as
regards DSD cases. We seek to recover costs from DSD as per the SLA-

In addition the actual outlay in respect of the conduct of SSA cases in the courts will be recharged to
the SSA. This includes the fees paid to external counsel in the prosecution of SSA cases. These will be
negotiated by the Assistant Director of Fraud & Departmental on the basis of fair and reasonable
remuneration for work necessarily performed. This will apply in respect of fees received from 1*
April 2010.

Initially we were trying to charge DSO for any separate fee submitted by Counsel for confiscation
proceedings. Lorraine Walls of DSD challenged this through Ann. Ann and I looked at the SLA and considered
that we could not seek to recoup from DSD any special fee sought by Counsel for confiscation. However in
practical terms Counsel mostly submitted a composite fee request for trial and confiscation and where there
was no separate amount claimed for confiscation DSD were invoiced for the total amount.

if there is to be a future SLA with Post Office and indeed all other Departments the issue of confiscation fees
would need to be addressed as per Jenny’s suggestion, and I understand that separate draft SLAs re Restraint
and Confiscation have already been drawn up by Ann.

I would suggest though that as we will have to draw up new SLAs with Departments anyway, as we are moving to a
full cost recovery basis, that we should have just one SLA which includes a section on restraint and

confiscation.
