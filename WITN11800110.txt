Mrs Dorothy Bontoft (

We wrote to you on 08/07/2021 advising you that the above named person
made an application to the CCRC for a review of their case. Mrs Bontoft was
convicted at Newcastle-upon-Tyne Crown Court on 17/09/2004 of false
accounting and theft and was sentenced to 21 months imprisonment, suspended for 2
years and ordered to pay £1500 towards cost of prosecution on 11/02/2005.

The application to the CCRC has now concluded and we are therefore
withdrawing the direction made under section 17 of the Criminal Appeal Act
1995 on 08/07/2021.

Please note that the CCRC has referred this case for an appeal. The Court will
schedule it for hearing in due course. Under sections 9 to 12 of the Criminal
Appeal Act 1995, the case is now treated as an appeal by the person who was
the subject of the review. Please note that the documents and other material
previously covered by our section 17 notice may now be called for by the Court
and you should address any further enquiries to them.

Thank you for your assistance in this matter.
