Subject: Agenda and papers: Board meeting (CCRC) 24th September 2020

Dear all,

The agenda and papers for Thursday’s Board meeting have been published on Diligent. Nick Vamos has provided the
following overview of the papers:

Current criminal appeals

The Board has now made decisions on stance in all the referred cases so draft Respondent’s Notices are attached for
the Board’s approval. There are two draft RNs to match the two Statements of Reasons issued by the CCRC. This
division is entirely procedural. Each RN is in two parts: (i) matters of general application including a generic response
to the CCRC’s reasoning; and (ii) case-specific analyses and decisions. The generic parts take up the first 13 pages and
are effectively the same in each RN. For each case, there is are a very short case summary followed by the reasons for
each decision. There is nothing new in the case summaries and the reasons are almost identical to the Record of
Decisions already seen and approved by the Board.

We will not be serving RNs in the Crown Court cases, which are only required if the appeal is being opposed. Instead,
the proposal is for each appellant to receive a letter setting out, in broad terms, the basis for POL’s decision. This will
place the Crown Court appellants on the same footing as the CACD appellants who will receive a similar explanation
in the RNs. Example drafts of the Crown Court letters are attached. The precise wording may be subject to
amendment depending on the final form of the press release but we attach two examples of the wording to be used
in Ashraf and Patel.

We also attach a finalised Record of Decisions including Parekh and Yates. A tracked change version is also attached
for reference. The summary table of decisions has also been updated and finalised.

Finally, we attach an updated timetable for the criminal appeals and subsequent civil claims. A separate
communications plan and time-table is being produced to manage the process up to and including service of the RNs
on 2 October.

Future criminal appeals
We have prepared a PowerPoint presentation on the management of future appeals. The Board is asked to approve
the recommendations for next steps on the final slide.

Potential claims against Fujitsu
We have prepared a slide summarising the potential claims against Fujitsu arising from the Horizon Issues judgment.

Miscellaneous

As promised last week, we attach a summary of counsel’s advice on Stamps, which may not require a separate agenda
item but of course counsel will be available to answer any questions arising.
