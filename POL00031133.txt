Introduction

A Programme reporting structure has been agreed and a process is in
place to capture and report month-end progress and issues. The
Report will be issued to the Horizon Board prior to their meetings
(currently scheduled to meet 16 June, 9 September and 26
November), and to the Horizon Delivery Group in those months that
the Board do not meet.

This report summarises the state of the Horizon Programme as at
May month end.

Programme Overview
The major focus of the Programme at the current time is:

+ supporting the existing live systems.

» brokering and obtaining agreement to the preferred option for the
Release 2 replan.

» monitoring the delivery of ICL Pathway’s Release 2 and future
releases.

» finalising and agreeing the Migration and Live Trial strategies.

Live Operations

The current Release continues to provide a Benefit Payment Service
and Order Book Control System for Child Benefit Payments in 204
outlets (119 in the South West & South Wales Region and 85 in the
North East Region). From an operational point of view, there are no
major threats to business continuity.

During April, there were over 78,000 encashments (including foreign
encashments) with a total value in excess of £2.2m.

Problems with lost transactions have not yet been resolved. ICL
Pathway have recognised the need to address concerns and system
fixes are being provided. These will concentrate on printer problems
and time-out issues that account for 70% of the lost transactions. A
reduction in lost transaction numbers should be seen in the
forthcoming months. This issue is regarded as a priority 1 level, and
the situation is being closely monitored.

Security Assurance

Pathway continue to make good progress across the broad security
front. Some, as yet, minor problems are being worked on regarding
the use of addresses for Extended Verification Procedure (EVP) but
progress at this stage is satisfactory

Business Assurance

We continue to work closely with Pathway and clarify any
remaining aspects of the business design. We support the process of
quality and compliance reviews against a broad variety of
deliverables including functional specifications, test preparation and
acceptance specifications.

A revised process has been agreed which is designed to accelerate
the delivery of user documentation into Model Office and Live Trial.
This will involve a move to integrated team working with Pathway
and is currently being documented .

Few issues remain, in advance of active testing, regarding
Automated Payments (AP) and Benefit Payment Service (BPS),
however, EPOSS continues to cause concern. There are 117
identified issues the majority of which can not be cleared until we
have confirmation, either logically in a document or physically on
the product itself, that the solution is acceptable.

15.3 Acceptance
The review of the Acceptance Process is planned to complete mid

June. The review is looking at the Acceptance process, content and
timescale, the co-ordination between Stakeholders and the Supplier
and resource / structure implications. The aim is to ensure that the
Pathway system can be accepted in a timely and satisfactory way.
Any recommendations will need to be taken into account before
overall timescales for the repian are finalised.

15.5 Service Management Development
Scoping and definition work has commenced as part of the
development of Configuration Management which is an essential
discipline that underpins Change Management.

Change Management is currently the subject of a number of
discussions both within POCL and with Pathway. Service
Management are to provide the formal interface to Pathway with
regard to (operational) change.

A methodology has been agreed to support the Operational Business
Change process and its formal connection to the terms of the
contract.

Current Position:

Following the April Post Office Board update a total review of the
Horizon Business Case has commenced. This work is reviewing both the
Base Case and the Automation Case, it should be noted that a full review
of the Base Case has not been carried out since 1995/6,

The revised Business Case will be presented to CoMPEC in July with the
aim of taking it to MaPEC in September.

2.2.2 Future Business Case Reporting:
Horizon Board reports will provide updates on the Horizon Business case
and associated Business Plan implications. This will take a feed from the
in year budget reporting, bat updates will be provided for all key
elements of the business case/plan (i.e. income & expenditure areas)
across the life of the programme. These updates will take due account of
changes in plans/assumptions/ volumes etc. and any authorised change
control notes. This report will also review the quantified financial risks
associated with the Programme.

The prime focus will be on the NPV of each business case area with
explanations of changes in forecasts and variances to the NPVs included
in the authorised case. The report will also comment on forecast
performance against the Total Authorised Sum.
