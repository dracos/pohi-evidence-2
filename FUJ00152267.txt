1 MANAGEMENT SUMMARY

Summary: Within the Fujitsu Services domain for December 06 there no Major Incidents, one service affecting
incident within the Fujitsu domain and 2 incidents relating to VIP sites. Details are given in the Incident
Overview below.

Branch and counter availability continue to remain above target at 99.8% and 99.2% respectively. Despite the
expected low volumes of OBC activity in the month, with 60 changes delivered, all the OBC targets were
exceeded with all OLAs met.

Incoming calls to the desk were lower given the shorter working month, and a reduction in inappropriate and
network related calls. However, hardware calls remain at the same relative level. The incoming call volumes
continue to exceed the agreed levels and the associated SLTs have been recalculated accordingly.

Counter printer volume was adversely affected by the problem identified with the replacement mails labels, and
revised instructions to branches were issued to attempt to alleviate this effect towards the end of December.
The resulting call peaks led to continued issues with Engineering SLTs and work continues to put a sustainable
recovery plan in place with achievable target dates.

A total of 85 complaints were received, 30 were passed to Fujitsu for information only, and 10 were for
investigation and comment. Of these, 1 was agreed to be justified and a further 3 are still under investigation.

Commentary

The number of complaints logged against Fujitsu Services remains constant, with similar numbers of complaints
passed to the HSD for information / investigation, although complaints against equipment & engineering and
printers have increased slightly against November.

There has been a slight increase in the number of complaints logged for counter printers which correlates with
the high number of faults reported at the end of November / beginning of December. The root cause of the
increase in counter printer faults is believed to be a change in the label specification, which is being investigated
by Post Office Ltd and Royal Mail Group. Due to these high call volumes, there was a period of 7 days where
there was a shortage of printers in the spares loop. The HSD were actively contacting branches to enquire if
they were impacted by the shortage and engineering prioritised single counter branches, however the impact of
the shortage will be seen in next month’s stats.

December saw solid down comms faults at an all time low, with an average of 7 branches impacted per week
across the whole network. Focus remains on intermittent faults, where online service can drop out and retum, to
varying degrees during the day or during the week. Intermittent faults are more difficult to diagnose as they can
often be caused by environmental issues both within or external to the branch - e.g. interference from a
neighbour's electrical equipment; an incorrectly filtered alarm or branch non-conformance to specified
configuration - such as overloading the phone line. Diagnosis of intermittent faults relies on ruling out potential
causes and engineers or technical experts need to be on site or monitoring at times when the fault occurs which
is very difficult when occurrence of the problem is random. Diagnosis is also problematic in that it may require
cooperation from a branch to allow the system to be taken down for a significant period of time in an effort to
isolate potential problems, which can inconvenience branch ability to serve customers unfortunately, this action
must be taken to restore service. A great deal of focus and attention is being put on intermittent fault resolution
processes over the next 3 months in a bid to reduce the resolution times with a joint POL Operations / Fujitsu
Services action plan being put in place.

5.2 Engineering Service

December saw a return to the number of visits experienced during October. There was a slight decrease in the
number of printer swaps, both BOH and CP and screen replacements also reduced slightly.

Although the Counter Printer volumes were down on those of November the quantities still remained high, a
large percentage of these failures have been attributed to an issue with a mail label. It has been identified that
the backing paper is of a different quality and results in labels becoming un-stuck, thus resulting in
contamination of the mechanical parts of the printer rendering it in need of repair.

As a result the SLT conformance for December has seen a performance of 64% for A priorities within 4 hours
and 88% for C priorities. If we remove the Ithaca failures as a result of the new labels then the C priority
conformance rises to 94% for the month. There are currently workshops underway to review and understand
the 4 hour call process and improve procedures resulting in a full detailed service improvement plan with clear
deliverables, action owners and time-scales.

The Epson to Oki and Panasonic to OKI back office printer replacement project is now complete.
The Royal Mail Group programme continues with phase 1 drawing to a close.

It is now confirmed that we expect the first of the Epson Counter Printers to be rolled into the estate during
March. It is expected that this will have a positive impact on a required reduction in call volumes going forward.

5.3 Branch Network Services / Central Network
Branch Network Services

During December the 837 Calls were logged across all Network Types into the Fujitsu Services. This is a
decrease against the previous 3 months.

The ‘Priority A — 6 hours’ SLT saw 14 failures from a total of 751 calls. Although the target (100%) was missed,
a result of 98.1% was achieved against 98.3% for November.

The Priority C - 6 hours’ SLT saw 2 failures from 62 calls. Although the target is (100%) was missed, a result of
96.8 was achieved.

SLA Failure, After investigation into the failures above we have identified that the contributing factors were
related to the HSD call handling and delays in engineering attendance. Both of these issues are being
addressed with the appropriate SDU’s.

Other issues in December

We have continued to receive a high number of ADSL/IP service cease notifications being processed by BT due
to Branch Manager's activity. We are continuing to progress this issue with assistance from POL, C&W and
internal SDU’s.

Over the past Month there has been an increase in Branches with no Trading/On-Line Services Capability with
2 Branches being without service for 6 days and 7 being without service for 4 days. This has been partially
attributed to high call volumes with FMS and is also attributed to Branches where the ADSL service has been
ceased and no back up Network availability has been available or the PM has declined the use of the PSTN line
for back service to be applied.

Central Network

In Dec-06 there were a total of 837 network related calls logged at the HSD which is the lowest number since
before CAPO was fully deployed in May-03. The cause code breakdown shows this result is due to no major or
significant incidents affecting branch trading inside the Fujitsu or other Post Office supplier domains.
