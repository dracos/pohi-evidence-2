“It’s about following your conscience”

Do you remember the Winterbourne View abuse scandal in 2012?

Six care workers at the care home were given prison sentences for “cruel, callous
and degrading" abuse of disabled patients.

Did you know, Terry Bryan, an experienced nurse, had raised his concerns about
the appalling abuse he witnessed at Winterbourne View to management?

He was ignored.

He turned to BBC Panorama to bring the care home’s conditions to the public’s
attention. Not only did Bryan’s whistleblowing of wrongdoing lead to prison
sentences, NHS England developed its 2011 “transforming care” agenda, aimed at
reducing patient admissions to care homes like Winterbourne.

When Bryan was asked if he would be prepared to blow the whistle again, it was a
decisive yes. For him, “it’s about following your conscience.” “How would you live
with yourself if you didn’t do it?”
